# 🎨 Quick Start: Generate 10 Test Characters

**Goal:** Generate 10 diverse character images optimized for Blender auto-rigging

**Time:** ~15 minutes (10 images × ~90 seconds each)

---

## 📋 Instructions

1. Open AI Studio: http://localhost:8000/ai-studio/
2. Navigate to **Text-to-Image** tab
3. Set these parameters:
   - **Model:** SD3 Large Turbo (fast!)
   - **Width:** 1024
   - **Height:** 1024
   - **Style:** 3D Model (or closest option)
4. Copy-paste each prompt below, one at a time
5. Click **Generate**
6. Wait ~90 seconds per image
7. Repeat for all 10 prompts

## ✅ After Generation

Once all 10 images are generated:

1. Navigate to **Image-to-3D** tab
2. Select each character image
3. Click **Generate 3D Model**
4. Wait for 3D conversion (~60 seconds each)

Then you'll have 10 3D models ready for Blender auto-rig testing!
