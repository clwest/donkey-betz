# Step 5: Week 4: Promote on social media

Below is a detailed, day-by-day action plan for Week 4: “Promote on social media” for your AI‑Generated Digital Templates opportunity. It uses only our internal platform tools and services, names specific internal services and specialist agents, and includes concrete steps, responsibilities, and expected outcomes. This plan emphasizes cost savings and seamless integration benefits of using our platform versus external tools.

Goal
- Drive awareness, traffic, and conversions for your AI‑Generated Digital Templates through coordinated organic and paid social promotion.
- Primary KPIs: impressions, engagement rate, link clicks (CTR), add-to-cart rate, conversions (template sales), and revenue. Secondary KPIs: follower growth and email signups.

Overview of internal tools to use (all internal, no external services)
- AI Content Studio (DALL·E 3 + Stable Diffusion) — create visuals, mockups, and short video frames.
- Design-Agent — produce branded graphics, carousels, and ad creative.
- Video-Agent — create short promo clips and animated reels.
- Content-Creator Agent — write post copy, captions, and CTAs optimized per platform.
- Marketing-Agent — build campaign strategy, audience segments, and copy tests.
- Publishing Automation System — schedule and publish posts across social channels, handle SEO metadata, and automate multi-platform distribution.
- Revenue Engine — generate product links, promo codes, payment checkout pages, and handle conversion funnels.
- ML Analytics & Optimization — track performance, run A/B tests, and provide optimization recommendations.
- Real-time Collaboration Tools — coordinate the team, review assets, and sign-off.
- Analytics-Agent / Growth-Agent — set tracking parameters, UTM standards, and retargeting lists.
- Coding-Agent (if needed) — integrate social pixels or landing page tweaks via platform API.

Why use only our platform
- Single integrated workflow from creative to sales and analytics reduces tool costs and manual handoffs.
- Native integrations (Publishing Automation ↔ Revenue Engine ↔ ML Analytics) enable more accurate attribution, faster optimization, and lower CAC.
- Use of specialized internal agents speeds production and keeps quality consistent without outsourcing.

Week 4 — Day-by-day plan

Day 1 — Kickoff, audience & messaging
Steps
1. Hold a 30–60 minute kickoff using Real-time Collaboration Tools with Marketing-Agent, Content-Creator, Design-Agent, and Analytics-Agent.
2. Define primary audiences and channels (e.g., Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest) based on prior data.
3. Agree on campaign pillars: product demo, template benefits, social proof, tutorial/how-to, limited-time promo.
4. Marketing-Agent drafts a content calendar and A/B test plan (2 headline variations × 2 image styles × 2 CTAs).
Tools/agents: Real-time Collaboration Tools; Marketing-Agent; Analytics-Agent.
Deliverables
- Content calendar for Week 4 (post cadence and platform mapping).
- A/B test matrix and KPI targets.
Expected outcomes
- Clear campaign brief and prioritized creative assets for production.

Day 2 — Create visuals & motion assets
Steps
1. Use AI Content Studio to generate initial hero images, mockups, and backgrounds (DALL·E 3 / Stable Diffusion prompts tailored to brand).
2. Design-Agent refines AI outputs into on‑brand graphics (post images, carousels, story templates).
3. Video-Agent uses the same studio assets to create 15–30 second promo reels and 6–10 second story clips.
4. Save all assets in the platform’s asset library for easy access.
Tools/agents: AI Content Studio; Design-Agent; Video-Agent.
Deliverables
- 6 static images (2 styles × 3 sizes).
- 3 carousel templates.
- 3 short videos (reel + story versions).
Expected outcomes
- A cohesive library of creative assets ready for copy integration and scheduling.

Day 3 — Copy, CTAs, and landing page setup
Steps
1. Content-Creator Agent writes captions, short-form copy for reels, alt text, and 2 CTA variations (e.g., “Browse templates” vs. “Get 20% off”).
2. Coding-Agent (if needed) integrates a lightweight landing section or updates product page with social-friendly metadata via platform API.
3. Revenue Engine: create dedicated campaign product links, one-click checkout pages, and promo codes for tracking conversions.
4. Analytics-Agent adds UTM parameters and configures ML Analytics tracking for each campaign link and creative variation.
Tools/agents: Content-Creator, Coding-Agent, Revenue Engine, Analytics-Agent, ML Analytics.
Deliverables
- Caption bank and CTAs for each platform and asset.
- Campaign-specific checkout links and promo codes.
- UTMs and tracking plan documented.
Expected outcomes
- Ready-to-publish content with conversion-ready links and tracking in place for accurate attribution.

Day 4 — Schedule & soft launch (organic)
Steps
1. Use Publishing Automation System to schedule organic posts across platforms at optimized times per audience (system recommends time slots).
2. Enable platform SEO metadata and auto-hashtag suggestions the Publishing Automation System provides.
3. Soft launch: publish an initial set of posts (one hero post + one reel +2 story updates) to test creative resonance.
4. Promote cross-channel by adding social posts to email footer or scheduled newsletter through publishing automation.
Tools/agents: Publishing Automation System; Content-Creator; Design-Agent.
Deliverables
- Scheduled posts for the week and a live soft-launch deployment.
Expected outcomes (24–48 hours)
- Early performance signals (impressions, engagement, CTR) to validate creative and copy. Expect measurable engagement and a small number of clicks to Revenue Engine links.

Day 5 — Paid boost & influencer/micro-partner outreach
Steps
1. Marketing-Agent identifies top-performing organic creative from Day 4 using early ML Analytics data.
2. Use the platform’s paid promotion workflow (via Publishing Automation System + Revenue Engine for tracking) to boost best-performing posts to target audiences. Use Revenue Engine campaign links so conversions are tracked directly.
3. Marketing-Agent coordinates outreach to 3–5 micro-influencers via Real-time Collaboration Tools; share asset packages and UTM-tagged links.
4. Set up retargeting segments in ML Analytics for users who clicked but didn’t convert.
Tools/agents: Publishing Automation System, Marketing-Agent, Revenue Engine, ML Analytics, Real-time Collaboration Tools.
Deliverables
- Live boosted posts and influencer outreach messages.
- Retargeting segments and pixel/UTM verification.
Expected outcomes (48–72 hours)
- Lift in impressions, clicks, and initial conversions. Expect CTR to increase vs organic baseline and first measurable revenue.

Day 6 — Monitor, analyze, and iterate
Steps
1. ML Analytics aggregates 72-hour performance across creative variants and channels. Analytics-Agent runs A/B test analysis and identifies winners.
2. Marketing-Agent and Content-Creator iterate copy and visuals for underperforming combos; Design-Agent prepares replacement assets as needed.
3. Re-allocate ad spend to top-performing creative and audience segments within the platform.
4. Publish an updated content batch via Publishing Automation System.
Tools/agents: ML Analytics, Analytics-Agent, Marketing-Agent, Content-Creator, Design-Agent, Publishing Automation System.
Deliverables
- A/B test report and updated creative lineup.
- Reallocated promotions to highest ROI creatives.
Expected outcomes
- Improved CTR and conversion rate; reduced CPA in real time due to reallocation.

Day 7 — Retarget, email blast, and reporting
Steps
1. Deploy retargeting ads to users who visited product pages but did not convert (ML Analytics + Publishing Automation).
2. Use Publishing Automation System to send a targeted email blast or social DM sequence to engaged users with a limited-time promo code created in Revenue Engine.
3. Compile a Week 4 performance report (ML Analytics dashboard + Revenue Engine revenue report) and hold a short debrief via Real-time Collaboration Tools.
4. Plan next-week optimizations and scale decisions based on results.
Tools/agents: ML Analytics, Revenue Engine, Publishing Automation System, Real-time Collaboration Tools, Analytics-Agent.
Deliverables
- Retargeting live and email/social DM campaign deployed.
- Final Week 4 report with recommendations for Week 5.
Expected outcomes
- Final conversion lift and clear insights: expected incremental conversions from retargeting and promo offers, and a data-driven plan for scaling.

A/B testing and optimization plan
- Test matrix: Image A vs Image B; Headline 1 vs Headline 2; CTA A vs CTA B.
- Use ML Analytics to run significance testing after 500+ impressions per variant (platform will recommend thresholds).
- Daily micro-iterations: swap out worst-performing variant each day until stable winners are found.

Tracking, attribution, and expected KPI ranges
- Implement UTM templates via Analytics-Agent to ensure clear attribution (channel, creative, audience, promo_code).
- Baseline KPI targets (adjust to account size/followers):
  - Impressions: 3,000–25,000 (depending on follower base and paid boost).
  - CTR (to landing page): 1.5%–6% (platform-optimized paid boosts expected to be higher).
  - Conversion rate (click → purchase): 1%–4% (improved by retargeting).
  - Revenue: depends on pricepoint; use Revenue Engine dashboards to monitor LTV/CAC.
- ML Analytics will provide predictive modeling to estimate expected conversions based on spend — use this to set daily bid caps.

Cost and ROI advantages of using our platform
- No external subscription integration required: creative, publishing, payments, and analytics are all native. This reduces tool licensing costs and eliminates cross-platform data reconciliation.
- Faster iteration loop (hours, not days) because assets, scheduling, and tracking live in the same system.
- Revenue Engine link + ML Analytics attribution will reduce misattributed conversions vs using separate ad platforms.

Final deliverables at end of Week 4
- Asset library (images, carousels, video clips) in platform storage.
- Content calendar and published posts record in Publishing Automation System.
- Promo codes and campaign checkout pages in Revenue Engine.
- ML Analytics performance report and A/B test results.
- Retargeting audiences and next-step recommendations.
