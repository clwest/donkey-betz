# Step 4: Week 3: List on Gumroad, Etsy, Creative Market

Summary
Week 3’s objective is to list your AI‑Generated Digital Templates for sale using our platform’s integrated tools — not external marketplaces. By using the Revenue Engine, AI Content Studio, Publishing Automation, ML Analytics, and our specialized agent network, you will create optimized product listings, set pricing/payment and delivery, prepare marketing assets, and launch the templates with tracking and optimization in place. This approach reduces fees, speeds setup, enables seamless cross-channel publishing, and gives you richer analytics than listing on Gumroad/Etsy/Creative Market separately.

High-level outcomes by end of Week 3
- Fully configured product pages for each template collection on our internal marketplace.
- Packaged downloadable files, license files, preview images and mockups generated with AI Content Studio.
- Pricing, payments, tax and delivery configured in the Revenue Engine, with upsells/bundles ready.
- Launch campaign scheduled via Publishing Automation (social, email, SEO), with initial tracking configured in ML Analytics.
- Baseline KPIs set (views, add-to-cart, conversion rate, revenue) and A/B test plan ready.

Week 3 Action Plan — Day-by-day steps, tools, responsibilities and expected outcomes

Day 1 — Prepare final product assets (3–4 hours)
1. Finalize product builds and file packaging
   - Action: Use design-agent and content-creator agents to confirm final templates (source files, exported formats like PNG, JPG, PDF, PSD, SVG, Figma, etc.) and create a standardized deliverable package (.zip).
   - Tool(s): Agent network (design-agent, content-creator) via internal API.
   - Output: One .zip per product variant containing templates + readme + license.txt + install instructions.
   - Expected outcome: 100% complete downloadable packages ready for upload.

2. Create license and usage terms
   - Action: Generate a clear license file and short EULA (personal vs commercial, allowed mods, attribution).
   - Tool(s): content-creator agent + legal-template library (internal).
   - Output: license.txt and a short “what’s allowed” snippet for the product page.

Day 2 — Generate visuals and copy (4–6 hours)
3. Create hero images, preview mockups, and thumbnail variations
   - Action: Use AI Content Studio (DALL·E 3 + stable diffusion templates) to generate 3 hero images and 6–8 preview mockups in required aspect ratios. Apply brand colors and consistent typography presets.
   - Tool(s): AI Content Studio; design-agent for refinements.
   - Output: 1 hero banner, 6 preview images, 1 thumbnail, and 2 variant thumbnails for A/B testing.
   - Expected outcome: Optimized visual assets for listing and social posts.

4. Write listing title, description, tags, and short bullets
   - Action: Use content-creator agent to produce SEO-optimized title, 3 sentence product summary, 7–10 keyword tags, 6 benefit bullets, and an FAQ snippet.
   - Tool(s): content-creator agent; ML Analytics keyword recommendations (internal).
   - Output: Final listing copy and tag list ready to paste into the listing builder.

Day 3 — Create product listing & pricing in Revenue Engine (3–4 hours)
5. Create the product entry in Revenue Engine
   - Action: Upload product packages and visuals to the Revenue Engine product manager. Enter title, description, tags, category, SKU, and attach license file.
   - Tool(s): Revenue Engine product manager (via platform dashboard/API).
   - Output: Draft product listing saved in Revenue Engine.

6. Set pricing, bundles, and upsells
   - Action: Configure base price, two-tier pricing (single license and commercial license), and a bundle option (e.g., 3 templates for X% off). Configure one-click upsell for a template bundle or extended license.
   - Tool(s): Revenue Engine pricing module and upsell builder.
   - Output: Pricing tiers and upsell workflows configured.
   - Expected outcome: Clear revenue paths and higher AOV (average order value).

7. Payment, tax and delivery settings
   - Action: Enable payment methods (cards, wallet), tax/VAT rules and compute rates for target regions, set delivery (instant downloads), download limits and expiration policies, and automated license delivery.
   - Tool(s): Revenue Engine payments + tax module; delivery settings in product manager.
   - Output: Payments enabled, taxes configured, and automated digital delivery validated with test checkout.
   - Expected outcome: Smooth checkout and instant, secure file delivery.

Day 4 — SEO, store page design, and QA (3–4 hours)
8. Configure SEO metadata and store placement
   - Action: Add SEO title, meta description, structured data (schema.org for digitalProduct), and choose store collection placement (e.g., “AI Templates > Social Media Templates”).
   - Tool(s): Revenue Engine listing SEO panel; Publishing Automation catalog management.
   - Output: SEO fields completed; listing assigned to collections.

9. Visual QA and functional testing
   - Action: QA team (design-agent + QA agent) performs: image compression check, layout preview on desktop & mobile, checkout test (sandbox), download extraction, license delivery, and cross-linking to support resources.
   - Tool(s): QA agent; internal testing sandbox environment; API test scripts.
   - Output: QA checklist signed off; bug/issue list (if any) resolved.
   - Expected outcome: Listing is error-free and ready for launch.

Day 5 — Launch planning and scheduling (3–4 hours)
10. Prepare launch assets and schedule distribution
    - Action: Create an email announcement and three social post variations (text + visuals) using content-creator and marketing-agent. Schedule release and promotional cadence (launch day, +3 days, +7 days).
    - Tool(s): Publishing Automation system; marketing-agent.
    - Output: Scheduled email and social posts, with suggested captions and hashtags.

11. Set up analytics & A/B testing
    - Action: Configure ML Analytics to track views, add-to-cart, conversion, revenue by variant, and run A/B tests on thumbnail and price tier for 7–14 days.
    - Tool(s): ML Analytics and A/B test manager (internal).
    - Output: Dashboard and alerts for KPI thresholds; A/B test configured to run automatically.
    - Expected outcome: Real-time insights into listing performance and a plan to iterate.

Day 6 — Launch and immediate monitoring (2–3 hours)
12. Go live
    - Action: Publish the product listing to the internal marketplace and trigger the scheduled email and social posts via Publishing Automation.
    - Tool(s): Revenue Engine publish action; Publishing Automation.
    - Output: Live product listing and launched marketing push.
    - Expected outcome: First traffic and orders within hours.

13. Monitor performance and respond to issues
    - Action: Use ML Analytics dashboards and alerting to monitor real-time metrics. Have customer-support-agent ready to respond to early buyers, and schedule a 24-hour check-in to resolve any user issues.
    - Tool(s): ML Analytics; customer-support agent; Revenue Engine order management.
    - Output: First 24hr performance report and any support tickets addressed.

Day 7 — Post-launch review & optimization plan (2–3 hours)
14. Initial performance review
    - Action: Collect data (views, CTR, add-to-cart, conversion rate, revenue, refunds, downloads) and compare to baseline targets. Use marketing-agent to propose immediate optimizations (change thumbnail, tweak copy, adjust price, promote coupon).
    - Tool(s): ML Analytics; marketing-agent; content-creator agent.
    - Output: 7‑day optimization roadmap with prioritized A/B tests.

15. Next steps and backlog
    - Action: Create backlog items for design improvements, additional template variants, and new bundles based on early customer feedback. Schedule tasks for Week 4.
    - Tool(s): Project management agent (internal).
    - Output: Prioritized Week 4 plan.

Roles & Responsibilities
- You / Product Owner: Approve final assets, pricing, and go-live.
- design-agent: Create and refine visuals and mockups.
- content-creator: Produce copy, FAQs, license files.
- marketing-agent: Build social/email sequences and launch plan.
- QA agent: Test downloads, checkout, and listing UX.
- customer-support agent: Handle early buyer inquiries and feedback.
- Revenue Engine (Platform Team): Payments, product publishing, upsells, and tax.
- ML Analytics: Tracking, A/B tests, and optimization recommendations.
All these run on internal API endpoints and dashboards to ensure one-source control.

Expected KPIs and Targets (first 7 days)
- Views: 500–2,000 (depending on audience)
- Add-to-cart rate: 3–6%
- Conversion rate: 1–3% (target initial, optimize toward 3–5% with A/B tests)
- Average order value: increase 10–25% with bundle/upsells
- Refund rate: <3%
