# Step 5: Week 4: Promote on social media

Overview
This action plan covers Week 4: Promote on social media for the AI-Generated Digital Templates opportunity. It uses only our internal platform tools and specialists to create assets, schedule and run campaigns, monitor performance, optimize in real time, and convert viewers into buyers. The plan is organized by day with specific steps, the internal tool or agent to use, expected deliverables, and measurable outcomes.

Goals and KPIs (for Week 4)
- Reach: 10,000 – 50,000 impressions across channels (depending on audience size).
- Engagement rate: 2%–6% (likes, comments, shares).
- Click-through rate (CTR) to product page: 1.5%–4%.
- Conversion rate from social traffic to purchase: 1%–3%.
- Revenue target: based on conversion rate × average product price via Revenue Engine (set a realistic baseline, e.g., $500–$3,000 for Week 4 depending on audience).
We will track these via ML Analytics & Optimization and Revenue Engine.

Pre-week setup (immediately before Week 4)
1. Finalize product pages and tracking.
   - Tools: Revenue Engine (create product, set pricing, coupon codes) + Revenue Engine API to generate trackable storefront links and UTM-tagged checkout URLs.
   - Deliverable: Live product page and at least 3 unique trackable URLs for social campaigns.
   - Expected outcome: Clean, tracked purchase flow so social traffic is attributed correctly.

2. Confirm creative theme, brand assets, and templates.
   - Tools: AI Content Studio + design-agent.
   - Deliverable: Brand kit (logo, color palette, fonts), product screenshots, hero images, and a short demo GIF/video.
   - Expected outcome: Consistent visuals ready for social posts and ads.

Week 4 Day-by-day Action Plan

Day 1 — Creative production and messaging
1. Generate primary visuals and variations.
   - Tools: AI Content Studio (DALL·E/Stable Diffusion) for hero images; Image/Video Pipeline for short demo videos (vertical for Reels/TikTok, square for Instagram/Facebook).
   - Tasks: Produce 3 hero images + 2 video cuts (10–30s) + 3 thumbnail options.
   - Deliverable: Asset library with versions A/B/C.

2. Create caption and copy variants.
   - Tools: content-creator agent.
   - Tasks: Produce 3 caption tones (educational, benefit-focused, urgency/launch-offer), 3 CTA variants, and hashtag sets.
   - Deliverable: Copy pack with 9 caption variants and hashtags tailored by channel.

3. Approvals and feedback.
   - Tools: Real-time Collaboration Tools (commenting, version control).
   - Deliverable: Approved asset set and final copy.

Expected outcomes: 10–15 post-ready assets and copy variations, approved and tagged for A/B testing.

Day 2 — Campaign setup, audience targeting, and scheduling
1. Define channel plan and posting cadence.
   - Tools: publishing automation system (channel connectors preconfigured).
   - Channels: Instagram Feed + Reels, Facebook Page, LinkedIn (if B2B), TikTok, Pinterest (assets), Twitter/X.
   - Cadence: 2 organic posts per channel this week + 1 boosted post per channel.

2. Build social campaigns and ad segments.
   - Tools: marketing-agent + publishing automation system (campaign builder and paid promotion module).
   - Tasks: Define audiences (interest, lookalike from existing customer lists via Revenue Engine), create 3 audience segments: Warm (site visitors), Lookalike (using Revenue Engine user data), Cold interest-based.
   - Deliverable: Campaign matrix with creatives mapped to audiences.

3. Schedule baseline organic posts.
   - Tools: publishing automation system API (schedule posts, set thumbnails, captions, and UTM links).
   - Deliverable: Organic posting schedule for the week with trackable links.

Expected outcomes: Published schedule with audiences and tracking in place to measure channel-specific performance.

Day 3 — Launch paid promotions and influencer outreach
1. Launch paid promotions (internal promotion engine).
   - Tools: publishing automation system paid promotion module + marketing-agent to implement and monitor.
   - Tasks: Start with small budgets for A/B tests (recommend $200–$600 total across segments; adjust to budget). Run two creative variants per audience.
   - Deliverable: Live boosted campaigns with budget allocation and scheduled optimization checks.

2. Micro-influencer and affiliate outreach.
   - Tools: marketing-agent + platform agent network (use influencer matching within the platform).
   - Tasks: Identify 5–10 micro-influencers who align with templates (offer affiliate links via Revenue Engine). Send templated outreach and negotiate simple content/discounts.
   - Deliverable: 2–5 confirmed micro-influencer posts with tracking links/codes.

Expected outcomes: Paid ads live and at least 1–2 influencer channels queued to drive traffic within the week.

Day 4 — Monitor, analyze early signals, and optimize
1. Real-time performance monitoring.
   - Tools: ML Analytics & Optimization dashboards + automated alerts.
   - Tasks: Check impressions, CTR, CPC, engagement, and early conversion data. Review heatmaps or behavior signals if available.
   - Deliverable: Day 4 performance snapshot and list of optimizations.

2. Optimization actions.
   - Tools: publishing automation system (pause/scale creatives), marketing-agent recommendations, A/B testing via the platform.
   - Tasks: Reallocate budget to top-performing variants, replace low-performing creative, refine audience targeting.
   - Deliverable: Optimized campaign allocations and updated creative set.

Expected outcomes: Improved CPA by 10–25% through early optimizations and clearer winners for Day 5 scaling.

Day 5 — Scale winners and refresh creative
1. Scale high-performing ads and posts.
   - Tools: publishing automation system paid promotion + marketing-agent.
   - Tasks: Double down on top audience/creative combinations. Increase daily spend on winners by 30–100% (within budget).
   - Deliverable: Scaled campaign deployments.

2. Refresh creatives for ad fatigue prevention.
   - Tools: AI Content Studio + design-agent + Image/Video Pipeline for new assets.
   - Tasks: Generate 2 new creative variations using the same messaging but different visuals/cut formats.
   - Deliverable: Replacement creatives scheduled for Day 6.

Expected outcomes: Maintain or improve CTR and conversions as reach expands.

Day 6 — Community engagement and content repurposing
1. Engage with audience and comments.
   - Tools: publishing automation system (inbox/engagement module) + real-time collaboration tools for team responses.
   - Tasks: Respond to comments, surface UGC for future use, pin high-performing comment or testimonial.
   - Deliverable: Engagement log and queued UGC assets.

2. Repurpose content into micro-formats.
   - Tools: Image/Video Pipeline + AI Content Studio.
   - Tasks: Create story-sized clips, a 30s product explainer, and 3 social cards highlighting benefits and testimonials.
   - Deliverable: Micro-content pack scheduled for the final posts.

Expected outcomes: Higher engagement and additional social assets for prolonged reach.

Day 7 — Week summary, reporting, and next-week plan
1. Generate performance report.
   - Tools: ML Analytics & Optimization + Revenue Engine analytics.
   - Tasks: Produce a consolidated report: impressions, reach, engagement, CTR, conversion, revenue, CPA, ROAS per channel and per creative.
   - Deliverable: PDF/interactive dashboard summary and insights.

2. Recommendations and next steps.
   - Tools: marketing-agent to synthesize insights; real-time collaboration for stakeholder sign-off.
   - Tasks: Identify top 3 performing channels/creatives, recommended budget for Week 5, and growth experiments (e.g., expanded influencer program, email retargeting).
   - Deliverable: Actionable Week 5 plan and prioritized backlog.

Expected outcomes: Clear data-driven decisions for scaling, and alignment on Week 5 focus areas.

Testing and Optimization Plan (ongoing during Week 4)
- A/B test variables: creative (visuals/video), caption/CTA, audience segment, post format (Reel vs. static).
- Use ML Analytics & Optimization to run automated winner selection (multi-armed bandit where available).
- Implement weekly automated alerts for CPA thresholds and engagement dips.

Monetization and Conversion Flow
- Use Revenue Engine to manage checkout, promo codes, and affiliate payouts. Link every social post to tracked Revenue Engine URLs to attribute conversions.
- Set up one-click checkout or friction-reducing landing experience for mobile users through the platform’s storefront integrations.

Roles and Agents (who does what)
- marketing-agent: overall campaign strategy, audience definitions, budget allocation, influencer outreach coordination.
- content-creator agent: captions, hashtags, copy variants, short scripts.
- design-agent + AI Content Studio: visuals, thumbnails, templates, and variant creation.
- Image/Video Pipeline: produce optimized video cuts for each platform format.
- publishing automation system: scheduling, paid-promote management, engagement inbox.
- ML Analytics & Optimization: dashboards, automated tests, alerts, and optimization recommendations.
- Revenue Engine: product links, tracking, coupon codes, affiliate management.
- Real-time Collaboration Tools: approvals, feedback loops, and content sign-off.

Expected Week 4 Metrics and Outcomes (conservative baseline)
- Impressions: 10k–25k.
- Clicks to product pages: 150–1,000.
- Purchases: 2–30 (depending on price and audience).
- Revenue: $200–$3,000 depending on conversion and price points.
- Learnings: Top-performing creative, best performing channel, optimal audiences for scaling.

Why use our internal platform (advantages)
- Cost savings: Consolidated tools reduce integration and subscription costs compared with using separate creative, scheduling, ad, analytics, and payment providers.
- Seamless tracking: Revenue Engine + publishing automation + ML Analytics provide end-to-end attribution out of the box via APIs, eliminating manual UTM setups and mismatch errors.
- Speed: AI Content Studio and the agent network produce creatives and copy faster than contracting externally.
- Optimization automation: ML Analytics automates A/B selection and budget reallocations, reducing manual overhead.
- Single-pane collaboration: Real-time Collaboration Tools keep approvals and feedback centralized, accelerating time-to-post.

Final deliverables for Week 4
- Asset library (images, videos, copy variants).
- Live organic schedule and active paid campaigns.
- Influencer/affiliate agreements with tracking links.
- ML Analytics dashboard and Week 4 performance report.
- Week 5 prioritized recommendations and budget proposal.
