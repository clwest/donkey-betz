# Step 1: Week 1: Create profiles on Upwork, Fiverr, Contently

Objective
Create professional, high-converting profiles and service listings that represent the “AI-Assisted Content Writing” offering — using only our internal platform tools and services (no external marketplaces). By the end of Week 1 you will have live internal “profiles” and service listings, a branded visual identity, a 5-piece portfolio of samples, pricing configured in the Revenue Engine, analytics tracking enabled, and a small outreach / publishing plan ready to activate.

Why use our internal platform (short)
- Single integrated workflow: profile, portfolio, content creation, payments, publishing and analytics are all connected via our platform APIs.
- Lower cost and faster onboarding vs external marketplaces because there are no third-party fees or platform restrictions.
- Access to specialized agents (design, content, marketing) and AI Content Studio for rapid, high-quality assets.

Week 1 Action Plan — Day-by-day steps, tools, and expected outcomes

Day 1 — Branding & Account Setup
Steps
1. Create your primary account on the platform and enable Organization/Business profile.
2. Use AI Content Studio to generate a logo, cover image, and a branded color palette and fonts.
3. Ask the design-agent to refine the logo, create a header image sized for service listings, and produce a simple one-page brand guide.
Tools to use
- Platform Account Manager (create org profile)
- AI Content Studio (DALL·E / Stable Diffusion)
- design-agent (specialized agent via agent network)
Expected outcomes
- Business profile created and verified.
- Final logo and header image ready (PNG/SVG), plus a brand guide PDF.

Day 2 — Core Positioning & Service Packages
Steps
1. Work with content-creator agent to draft three service packages (e.g., Basic: 500-word blog post with AI editing; Standard: 1,000-word SEO optimized article + metadata; Premium: Content series + revision + performance report).
2. Define deliverables, turnaround times, revision policy, and scope for each package.
3. Use marketing-agent to craft 3-4 short value propositions / taglines and target client personas.
Tools to use
- content-creator agent
- marketing-agent
Expected outcomes
- Three clearly defined service packages with features and deliverables.
- 3 customer personas and taglines for use in listings and outreach.

Day 3 — Create Service Listings / Profiles (Internal Platform)
Steps
1. Use the platform’s service listing builder to create 3 service listings matching the packages defined on Day 2.
2. Populate title, short descriptions, long descriptions (SEO-friendly), FAQs, delivery time, and included extras.
3. Upload brand assets from Day 1 and add sample thumbnails generated by AI Content Studio.
4. Link each listing to the corresponding pricing object in the Revenue Engine (see Day 4).
Tools to use
- Service Listing Builder (internal publishing automation)
- AI Content Studio (thumbnails)
Expected outcomes
- 3 published internal service listings with visuals, descriptions, and FAQs.
- Listings are ready to accept orders or inquiries.

Day 4 — Pricing, Payments & Legal
Steps
1. Configure pricing tiers and trial/intro offers in Revenue Engine.
2. Connect bank account/payment gateway in Revenue Engine and set tax/invoice settings.
3. Upload terms of service and a privacy policy (use content-creator and legal-agent to draft templates).
4. Enable automated invoicing and receipt emails.
Tools to use
- Revenue Engine (pricing objects, payment processing)
- content-creator agent (copy)
- legal-agent (contract templates)
Expected outcomes
- Payment processing fully configured.
- Pricing for each package is live and tied to listings.
- Legal templates uploaded and automation enabled.

Day 5 — Portfolio & Samples
Steps
1. Use AI Content Studio to generate initial content samples (5 pieces), each tailored to your target personas and SEO keywords.
2. Have content-creator agent edit and humanize the AI-generated drafts, ensuring quality and coherence.
3. Publish these samples to your portfolio section in the platform and link them to the relevant service listings.
Tools to use
- AI Content Studio
- content-creator agent
- Service Listing / Portfolio module (publishing automation)
Expected outcomes
- 5 polished portfolio pieces published.
- Portfolio linked to service listings for credibility.

Day 6 — Messaging Templates, Workflow & Automation
Steps
1. Create standard client messaging templates: initial inquiry response, proposal template, onboarding questionnaire, and delivery email. Use content-creator agent to draft the text.
2. Set up the client onboarding automation: auto-send onboarding questionnaire once a gig is purchased or a lead converts.
3. Configure a basic workflow for delivery: draft -> client review -> revision -> final delivery.
Tools to use
- content-creator agent
- Publishing automation (workflow builder)
- Revenue Engine (order triggers)
Expected outcomes
- 4+ polished message templates.
- Onboarding automation connected to service orders.
- Defined delivery workflow implemented.

Day 7 — Analytics, Launch Prep & Soft Launch
Steps
1. Configure ML Analytics to track listing views, CTR, conversion rate, time-to-first-reply, average order value, and retention.
2. Create dashboard widgets and weekly reports to monitor these KPIs.
3. Use publishing automation and marketing-agent to schedule a soft launch: an email to your existing contacts and a multi-platform distribution blast (social posts, newsletter) promoting 1-2 featured listings.
4. Perform a final quality check; run a test order to ensure payment, delivery workflow, and analytics tracking work end-to-end.
Tools to use
- ML Analytics (dashboard & reports)
- marketing-agent
- Publishing automation (distribution)
- Revenue Engine (test payment)
Expected outcomes
- Analytics dashboard set up with baseline metrics.
- Soft launch scheduled and executed.
- Test order completed successfully.

Deliverables at the end of Week 1 (concrete)
- Verified business profile and branding package (logo, header, brand guide).
- 3 live internal service listings for AI-Assisted Content Writing.
- 5 portfolio samples published.
- Revenue Engine configured with payment gateway and pricing.
- Messaging templates and onboarding automation in place.
- ML Analytics dashboard tracking core KPIs.
- Soft launch completed and test order validated.

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) & Success Criteria for Week 1
- Profiles/listings live and discoverable internally: 100%.
- Portfolio pieces published: 5.
- Payment gateway connected and a successful test transaction: 1.
- Analytics events tracking listing views and conversions configured: 100%.
- Initial inquiries or test orders from soft launch: 3+ (target; adjust based on audience size).

Who to involve (recommended internal agents & roles)
- design-agent: logo, headers, thumbnails.
- content-creator agent: listing copy, portfolio editing, templates.
- marketing-agent: taglines, soft launch campaign.
- legal-agent: TOS and privacy policy.
- Revenue Engine admin: payment setup & pricing rules.
- ML Analytics specialist: dashboard configuration.

Integration & API notes
- Ensure service listings are linked to pricing objects in the Revenue Engine via the platform API so orders trigger the Revenue Engine workflows automatically.
- Instrument key pages/listings with ML Analytics event tags (listing_view, add_to_cart/convert, order_complete) via the platform API to enable real-time dashboards.
- Store branding and asset metadata in the platform asset library so design-agent and publishing automation can reuse them programmatically.

Risks & Mitigation
- Risk: AI drafts require heavy editing. Mitigation: Use content-creator agent to humanize and review before publishing.
- Risk: Payment configuration delays. Mitigation: Start Revenue Engine onboarding on Day 1 and schedule a dedicated session with a Revenue Engine admin.
- Risk: Low initial visibility. Mitigation: Use marketing-agent’s distribution automation and offer limited-time intro pricing to drive early traction.

Next steps after Week 1 (short)
