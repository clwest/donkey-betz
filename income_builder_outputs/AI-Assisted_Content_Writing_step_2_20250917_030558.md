# Step 2: Week 1: Build portfolio with 3 sample articles

Below is a practical, day-by-day action plan for Week 1: “Build portfolio with 3 sample articles” as part of developing the AI-Assisted Content Writing opportunity. This plan uses only our internal platform tools and agents, and it gives specific steps, tool assignments, time estimates, checkpoints, and expected outcomes.

Summary goal
- Produce, design, and publish three high-quality sample articles (800–1,200 words each) on your portfolio site, fully optimized and tracked using internal platform tools. Each article will include a hero image, SEO metadata, internal CTA to the AI-Assisted Content Writing service, and analytics tracking.

High-level deliverables by end of Week 1
- 3 completed articles published to your portfolio (live or staged)
- 1 hero image + 1 supporting image per article (generated/optimized)
- SEO metadata and on-page optimization for each article
- A/B headline test set up for at least one article
- Analytics dashboard and baseline performance metrics
- Reusable article template and production workflow saved in the system

Day-by-day action plan (detailed)

Day 1 — Strategy, topic selection, and briefs (2–3 hours)
1. Kickoff meeting (30–45 minutes) using Real-time Collaboration Tools.
   - Attendees: project lead, content-creator agent, marketing-agent, design-agent, and optional client/stakeholder.
   - Outcome: final approval of three topics and audience/purpose for each article.
2. Select article topics and goals (examples):
   - Article A: “How AI-Assisted Writing Boosts Small Business Content Productivity”
   - Article B: “5 Prompt Strategies to Get Better AI-Generated Blog Posts”
   - Article C: “AI-Assisted Content Workflow: A Step-by-Step Case Study”
3. Create content briefs using the content-creator agent. Each brief should include:
   - Target audience, primary keyword, secondary keywords, target word count (800–1,200), tone, CTA, and required sections.
   - Assign brief creation to content-creator agent via API call and store briefs in the project workspace.
4. Expected outcomes: 3 approved briefs uploaded to the workspace and visible to all agents and reviewers.

Day 2 — First drafts (3–4 hours)
1. Use the content-creator agent to generate first drafts for all three articles. Request:
   - SEO-friendly headlines (3 variants each), intro, subheadings, body content, conclusion, and CTA.
   - Internal linking suggestions to your site.
2. Generate readability and SEO baseline using ML Analytics & Optimization:
   - Readability target: Flesch score ~60–70 (friendly, professional).
   - Keyword density suggestions and meta title suggestions.
3. Reviewer (human or editing-agent) performs quick pass for factual accuracy and tone.
4. Expected outcomes: 3 first-draft documents in the collaboration workspace with suggested headlines.

Day 3 — Editing and SEO optimization (3–4 hours)
1. Assign content-creator agent + editing-agent to revise drafts based on reviewer feedback.
   - Tighten structure, ensure natural keyword use, improve transitions.
2. Use ML Analytics to run SEO optimization checks for:
   - Title length, meta description (150–160 chars), H1/H2 structure, image alt text suggestions, schema markup suggestions.
3. Marketing-agent drafts CTA copy to direct readers to the AI-Assisted Content Writing demo/signup (use Revenue Engine ready link later).
4. Expected outcomes: 3 articles at near-final draft stage with SEO checklist completed.

Day 4 — Images, graphics, and article design (2–3 hours)
1. Use AI Content Studio (DALL·E 3 / Stable Diffusion) to generate hero images and supporting images aligned to the article topics.
   - Provide the content-creator/ design-agent with the article briefs and desired image styles.
2. Use Image/Video Pipeline to:
   - Optimize image size and format for web (responsive sizes), generate alt text automatically, and produce a small social share card image.
3. Design-agent creates the article layout template (header image placement, pull quotes, CTA block).
4. Expected outcomes: Optimized hero and supporting images for each article and a saved article template.

Day 5 — Final edits, accessibility, and metadata (2–3 hours)
1. Final read-through in Real-time Collaboration Tools with stakeholders for approvals.
2. Finalize metadata (meta titles, descriptions), canonical URLs, and structured data markup using marketing-agent with ML Analytics recommendations.
3. Ensure accessibility: alt text, captioning where needed, and readable contrast via design-agent checks.
4. Expected outcomes: Approved final article files with full metadata and accessibility checks passed.

Day 6 — Integrate and format for publication (2–3 hours)
1. Use coding-agent to format and integrate articles into your portfolio/CMS via internal API, or prepare staged pages if publishing later.
   - Use the platform’s publishing templates for consistent layout and fast deployment.
2. Hook up Revenue Engine CTA endpoints on each article (for demo sign-ups or lead capture). This keeps monetization/lead tracking integrated without external services.
3. Set up UTM parameters for future campaign tracking via publishing automation.
4. Expected outcomes: Articles staged or published in the CMS with CTAs connected to Revenue Engine.

Day 7 — Publish, A/B test, and analytics setup (2–3 hours)
1. Publish all three articles using the publishing automation system (schedule or immediate publish).
2. Use ML Analytics to:
   - Create dashboards to track visits, time on page, CTR on CTAs, social shares, and engagement.
   - Configure an A/B headline test for at least one article (use platform’s A/B test feature).
3. Marketing-agent schedules social shares and an email blast using publishing automation.
4. Expected outcomes: Three live articles, analytics dashboards created, initial A/B test set running, and social/email distribution scheduled.

Roles and tool assignments (concise)
- Content-creator agent: Drafts, revisions, and final copy.
- Editing-agent: Proofreading and quality control.
- Design-agent: Layout templates, image placement, and accessibility checks.
- AI Content Studio: Generate hero/support images and social cards.
- Image/Video Pipeline: Image optimization and format handling.
- Coding-agent: CMS integration and API deployment.
- Marketing-agent: CTAs, metadata, and distribution scheduling.
- ML Analytics & Optimization: SEO scoring, A/B testing, dashboards.
- Real-time Collaboration Tools: Approvals, reviews, and stakeholder alignment.
- Publishing automation: Scheduling and multi-channel distribution.
- Revenue Engine: Connect CTAs and lead capture (no external payment tools needed).

Quality & acceptance criteria (what “done” looks like)
- Each article is 800–1,200 words, with a targeted Flesch readability score of ~60–70.
- Headlines: 3 tested variants per article; at least one article running an A/B test.
- SEO: Meta title and meta description present and optimized; H1 + H2 structure verified by ML Analytics.
- Images: One hero image and one supporting image per article, optimized and accessible (alt text).
- CTA: Each article includes a working CTA connected to the Revenue Engine lead/demo flow.
- Analytics: Dashboard created for article performance and baseline metrics captured.
- Template: Article design template saved for reuse.

Expected outcomes and KPIs to track first 30 days
- Live samples: 3 portfolio articles published.
- Initial KPIs to monitor: pageviews, average time on page (target 2+ minutes), CTA click-through rate (target 2–5%), bounce rate, social shares, and A/B test headline uplift.
- Platform benefits: faster production cycle (days vs. weeks), no external subscriptions, single integrated workflow for content → images → publishing → analytics → monetization.

Cost & integration benefits (why use our platform)
- Full-stack internal solution eliminates costs of multiple external subscriptions (design tools, image providers, A/B test tools, analytics platforms, and payment processors).
- Seamless API integration means frictionless handoffs between drafting, design, publishing, analytics, and monetization—reducing manual work and time-to-publish.
- Reusable templates and saved workflows speed up future content production and reduce marginal cost per article.

Next steps after Week 1
- Review analytics weekly and iterate on headlines, CTAs, and on-page copy using ML Analytics insights.
- Scale: replicate the saved template and workflow to produce 2–3 articles per week.
- Convert highest-performing sample article into a lead magnet or gated case study using Revenue Engine for rapid lead capture.
