# Step 3: Week 2: Create 3 demo automations

## 🎯 Objective
**Action Plan for Week 2: Create 3 Demo Automations**

**Objective:** Develop three demo automations to showcase the capabilities of our No-Code AI Automation opportunity using our internal platform tools.

---

### Step 1: Identify Use Cases for Automation
- **Action:** Collaborate with stakeholders to identify three specific processes or tasks within the business that can benefit from automation. These could include lead generation, content distribution, or customer follow-up.
- **Expected Outcome:** A clear understanding of the three targeted automation processes, ensuring they align with business needs and demonstrate the value of our no-code solutions.

### Step 2: Design Automation Workflows
- **Action:** Utilize our internal tools to map out the workflows for each automation. This can be done through our real-time collaboration tools, where team members can contribute ideas and feedback.
- **Expected Outcome:** Detailed workflow diagrams for each automation, outlining the steps involved and the expected inputs and outputs.

### Step 3: Utilize Specialized Agents for Workflow Creation
- **Action:** Engage our specialized agents, specifically the automation-agent, to assist in creating the automation workflows. This agent can provide insights on best practices and ensure the automations are efficient and effective.
- **Expected Outcome:** Completed automation workflows that are ready for testing, benefiting from expert input and industry standards.

### Step 4: Implement Automations with Internal Tools
- **Action:** Use our internal platform capabilities to build the automations. This will involve:
  - Leveraging our **AI Content Studio** to automate content generation where applicable.
  - Utilizing our **ML Analytics & Optimization** tools to set up tracking and measurement for the automations.
  - Integrating with our **Revenue Engine** for any monetization-related automations.
- **Expected Outcome:** Fully functional automations that are built using our seamless internal tools, ensuring cost savings and integration efficiency.

### Step 5: Test and Refine Automations
- **Action:** Conduct thorough testing of each automation to ensure they work as intended. Use our real-time collaboration tools to gather feedback from team members and make necessary adjustments.
- **Expected Outcome:** Optimized automations that are reliable and ready for demonstration, showcasing their effectiveness and efficiency.

### Step 6: Prepare Demo Presentation
- **Action:** Create a presentation that outlines the purpose, process, and impact of each automation. Use our internal design-agent to help create visually appealing slides that highlight key features and benefits.
- **Expected Outcome:** A polished demo presentation that effectively communicates the value of the automations, ready for internal or external stakeholders.

### Step 7: Schedule Demo Sessions
- **Action:** Organize and schedule demo sessions to showcase the three automations to relevant stakeholders. Utilize our publishing automation system to manage invitations and follow-ups.
- **Expected Outcome:** Engaged stakeholders who understand the potential of the No-Code AI Automation opportunity and are excited about its implementation.

---

By following this action plan, we will successfully create three demo automations that not only demonstrate the capabilities of our internal platform tools but also provide a clear vision of how these solutions can enhance business processes. The use of our internal tools ensures cost-effectiveness and seamless integration, maximizing the benefits for our organization.
