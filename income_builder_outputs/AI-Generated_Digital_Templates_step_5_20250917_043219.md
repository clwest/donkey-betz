# Step 5: Week 4: Promote on social media

Below is a focused, actionable Week 4 plan for “Promote on social media” as part of launching the AI‑Generated Digital Templates opportunity. All recommended tools and services are internal to the platform. The plan covers specific steps, which internal services to use, roles/agents to assign, tracking/optimization, and expected outcomes.

Goal for Week 4
- Drive awareness, traffic, and initial sales of the AI‑Generated Digital Templates product. Primary outcomes: impressions, social traffic to the product page, email signups, and initial conversions through the Revenue Engine.

Summary of tools and teams to use (internal only)
- AI Content Studio (DALL·E 3 / Stable Diffusion) — create visuals, mockups, and thumbnail images.
- Image/Video Pipeline — process and optimize images and short-form videos for each platform.
- Design-Agent — create on‑brand templates, carousel assets, and hero images.
- Content-Creator Agent — write captions, CTAs, and microcopy for posts and stories.
- Video-Agent (subset of 102+ agents) — produce short reels, vertical videos, and animated demos.
- Marketing-Agent — strategy, paid boost management, influencer/micro‑partner outreach.
- Publishing Automation System — schedule and publish posts across platforms, manage UTM parameters and link tracking.
- Revenue Engine — generate product links, coupon codes, checkout flows, and attach affiliate/referral links.
- ML Analytics & Optimization — monitor campaign performance, run A/B tests, and recommend reallocations in real time.
- Collaboration Tools — assign tasks, review assets, and provide approvals in real time.

Pre-week (day 0): Preparation (1–2 hours)
- Create campaign brief in Collaboration Tools and assign roles to Design-Agent, Content-Creator, Video-Agent, and Marketing-Agent.
- Use Revenue Engine to create the product landing page, a tracked purchase link, and a 10% launch coupon. Generate API-accessible link and short URL.
- Set naming conventions for UTM tags that Publishing Automation System will append (channel, creative, variant).
Expected outcome: assets clear, product links ready, team aligned.

Detailed Day-by-day Week 4 plan

Day 1 — Launch creative and copy production
- Design-Agent uses AI Content Studio to generate hero visuals, template mockups, and platform-specific sizes (Instagram square, Story/reel vertical, Facebook post, LinkedIn hero, Pinterest).
- Video-Agent uses AI Content Studio + Image/Video Pipeline to create: 15–30s reel showing template customization, 6–10s animated thumbnail for ads, and a 60s demo for LinkedIn/YouTube.
- Content-Creator drafts post captions for each platform, 3 headline variants for A/B tests, and short CTAs: “Try template now — link in bio,” and “Get 10% off with LAUNCH10.”
- Marketing-Agent configures initial paid boost plan (small budget test), influencer outreach list (3–5 micro-influencers), and audience targeting suggestions.
Tools: AI Content Studio, Design-Agent, Video-Agent, Content-Creator, Image/Video Pipeline, Marketing-Agent.
Expected outcome: full creative set (images + videos) and copy drafts ready for review.

Day 2 — Approvals, asset finalization, tracking setup
- Use Collaboration Tools to review and approve creatives. Design-Agent makes final refinements.
- Publishing Automation System: create campaign entries for each platform and attach UTM parameters and the Revenue Engine's tracked product link. Create two creative variants per platform for A/B testing (headline A vs headline B).
- Revenue Engine: set up conversion webhook/API so ML Analytics can ingest purchase events in real time.
Tools: Collaboration Tools, Publishing Automation System, Revenue Engine, ML Analytics & Optimization.
Expected outcome: scheduled content, tracking configured, A/B variants set.

Day 3 — Soft launch (organic posts + newsletter)
- Publish organic posts across prioritized platforms (Instagram feed + stories + reel, Facebook page post, LinkedIn post, Pinterest pin, X post), using Publishing Automation System to schedule and publish simultaneously.
- Send an email blast using Publishing Automation System’s email automation to the existing list, linking to the product via Revenue Engine link and using the LAUNCH10 coupon.
- Marketing-Agent begins influencer outreach through the agent network; negotiate single-post swaps or affiliate codes using Revenue Engine link.
Tools: Publishing Automation System, Revenue Engine, Marketing-Agent.
Expected outcome: initial impressions, site traffic spike, and early purchase/sign-up signals.

Day 4 — Paid test and real-time optimization
- Launch paid tests (small budget) managed by Marketing-Agent across best-performing channels from Day 3 (e.g., boost reel on Instagram, promote LinkedIn post).
- ML Analytics monitors top-line metrics hourly (impressions, CTR, CPC, add-to-cart, conversion). Set automated rule: if CTR > benchmark (e.g., 1.5%) and conversion rate > target (e.g., 2%), scale budget by X%.
- A/B test evaluation: let variants run 48 hours, but check early signals for creative winners.
Tools: Marketing-Agent (ad setup), ML Analytics & Optimization, Publishing Automation System.
Expected outcome: early performance data to inform budget distribution and creative winners identified.

Day 5 — Content refresh & community engagement
- Produce 2 follow-up posts: customer use-case mockup and behind-the-scenes “how it was made” short video created by Video-Agent.
- Marketing-Agent responds to comments and DMs (or sets up auto-responders for FAQs) and pushes micro-influencer posts live.
- Use Revenue Engine to create a short-term referral incentive for purchasers (e.g., share link, get 15% off next purchase).
Tools: Video-Agent, Publishing Automation System, Marketing-Agent, Revenue Engine.
Expected outcome: deeper engagement, social proof content deployed, referral funnel established.

Day 6 — Deep analysis and retargeting setup
- ML Analytics runs a 3-day performance summary and highlights top-performing audiences, creatives, and platforms.
- Marketing-Agent sets up retargeting segments in the Publishing Automation System for users who visited product page but did not convert; create a carousel ad with countdown-reminder creative.
- Design-Agent prepares urgency creatives (limited-time banner, last-chance story) using AI Content Studio.
Tools: ML Analytics, Publishing Automation System, Design-Agent, AI Content Studio.
Expected outcome: retargeting campaign ready, improved conversion likelihood, prioritized creative library.

Day 7 — Optimize, report, and plan next steps
- Reallocate remaining paid budget to best-performing creative/platform using Marketing-Agent recommendations.
- Use ML Analytics to produce a concise campaign report: impressions, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversion rate, revenue, top creatives, and recommendations for Week 5.
- Hold a brief review using Collaboration Tools to approve Week 5 focus: scale winners, create UGC/influencer case studies, or expand to additional channels.
Tools: ML Analytics, Marketing-Agent, Collaboration Tools, Revenue Engine (sales numbers).
Expected outcome: final performance report for the week and decision-tree for scaling.

KPIs and expected outcomes (benchmarks to monitor)
- Impressions: measurable reach across channels. Expect a measurable uplift in week-over-week social impressions; initial target: 10k–50k impressions depending on follower base and paid budget.
- Click-through rate (CTR): 1.0–2.5% across social posts (platform-dependent). Aim to identify creatives achieving CTR above baseline within 48 hours.
- Conversion rate (site visitors → purchase): 1.5–4% expected for targeted traffic from social to the Revenue Engine checkout, with higher rates for retargeted visitors.
- Revenue: goal is at least initial product sales to validate demand — target 10–50 purchases in Week 4 depending on audience size and paid spend (adjust to your org’s baseline).
- New emails/subscribers: +3–10% of existing list per launch (email signup CTA in posts and retargeting).
- Influencer impact: at least 3 micro-influencer placements or trials, tracked via Revenue Engine affiliate links.

Automation, measurement, and API integration notes
- Use the Publishing Automation System API to push content, schedule posts, and auto-append UTM parameters for consistent tracking.
- Connect Revenue Engine purchase webhooks to ML Analytics so revenue, LTV, and conversion funnels are recorded in real time for optimization rules.
- Use the platform’s ML Analytics API to feed performance signals to Marketing-Agent for automated budget reallocation.
- All creatives, revisions, and approvals flow through Collaboration Tools to ensure auditability and speed.

Cost savings and integration benefits (why internal tools)
- Using AI Content Studio + Design-Agent reduces creative procurement cost vs. external agencies and external design subscriptions. The platform consolidates image/video generation and editing in one workflow.
- Publishing Automation System combined with ML Analytics eliminates the cost and friction of third‑party scheduling and analytics tools while enabling real‑time optimization and automated budget reallocation through internal APIs.
- Revenue Engine handles checkout, couponing, and affiliate/referral links, removing the need for external ecommerce platforms and preserving higher margins and consolidated reporting.
- The 102+ agent network centralizes specialized tasks (design, video, content, marketing) under a single billing and collaboration system for faster turnaround and lower overhead.

Final deliverables by end of Week 4
- Full creative library (images, videos, carousel variants) optimized for major platforms.
- Live social campaign (organic + paid) with UTM-tagged links to Revenue Engine checkout.
- Active retargeting segments and referral program set up.
- ML Analytics campaign performance dashboard and a Week‑4 performance report with recommendations for Week 5 scaling.
