5) Common pitfalls & how to avoid them
- Using Canva Pro-only elements: filter elements by “Free only” or check for the “Pro” badge. Replace Pro assets before packaging.
- Broken editability: flattening text into images or grouping images so buyers can't edit. Use text boxes, avoid flattened PNGs for editable areas.
- Copyright/licensing mistakes: don’t include paid stock images unless license allows resell. Use free stock or Canva’s free library.
- No instructions: buyers need a one-page “How to use” PDF. Include steps to make a copy and edit fonts/colors.
- Poor export resolution: export preview images at too low resolution — use 2000 px for hero images.
- Overcomplicated edits: don’t build overly nested groups; keep layers simple and clearly labeled.
- Not checking mobile view: preview templates on a phone inside Canva to ensure text legibility.
