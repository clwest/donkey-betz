# Step 4: Week 3: Offer services to local businesses

Goal for Week 3
Offer AI-Powered Social Media Management services to local businesses, sign initial paying clients (or pilots), and validate pricing and delivery workflows using only our internal platform tools. Deliverables by end of Week 3: outreach executed, 8–15 qualifying meetings, 3–6 pilot clients signed (or 1–3 paid clients), and initial campaigns launched and measured.

High-level approach
- Use our internal agent network to create sales materials and outreach campaigns.
- Use Revenue Engine to handle proposals, contracts, and payments.
- Use AI Content Studio + Image/Video Pipeline + design-agent to produce marketing deliverables and campaign assets.
- Use Publishing Automation to run campaigns and ML Analytics & Optimization to measure impact and optimize.
- Use Real-time Collaboration Tools for onboarding, project management, and client communication.

Week-3 Day-by-day action plan (5 business days)

Day 1 — Prep: Package, pricing, and content toolkit
1. Define 3 service packages (use Revenue Engine to publish them):
   - Basic (Social Presence) — monthly: social posting (12 posts), 1 platform, caption generation, performance report.
   - Growth (Engagement & Ads) — monthly: 20 posts across 2 platforms, 1 short promo video, light paid amplification, biweekly reporting.
   - Premium (Lead Generator) — monthly: 30 posts across 3 platforms, 2 promo videos, lead capture funnels, conversion optimization, weekly reports.
   - Set clear deliverables, SLAs (posting cadence, turnaround times), and trial/pilot terms (e.g., 30-day pilot at 50% off).
2. Create price points and projected margins using Revenue Engine’s pricing templates (publish as selectable plans via Revenue Engine API).
3. Use content-creator and design-agent to build a sales deck, one-page service sheet, and 3 sample post templates for each package using AI Content Studio for imagery and Image/Video Pipeline for short video templates.
Tools: Revenue Engine (plans + API), content-creator, design-agent, AI Content Studio, Image/Video Pipeline, Real-time Collaboration Tools.
Expected outcome: Published service packages, sales collateral, and 3 ready sample social posts per package.

Day 2 — Prospect list + outreach campaign build
1. Build a targeted local prospect list (20–50 businesses) focusing on verticals with clear social ROI (cafes, salons, fitness studios, boutique retail, professional services).
2. Use marketing-agent to draft multi-touch outreach: email, LinkedIn DM, and local community message templates. Include a short offer: free 30-minute audit + limited-time pilot pricing.
3. Use Publishing Automation to schedule local social ads (hyper-local targeting) driving to a landing page created by coding-agent that describes the service and captures leads. Use Revenue Engine checkout on that landing page.
4. Load outreach sequences into Real-time Collaboration Tools for tracking.
Tools: marketing-agent, coding-agent, Publishing Automation, Revenue Engine, Real-time Collaboration Tools.
Expected outcome: Outreach sequences live, landing page published, and ad test running.

Day 3 — Active outreach and qualification
1. Start outbound outreach: send emails, DMs, and calls. Track outcomes in Real-time Collaboration Tools CRM.
2. Offer 30-minute discovery/mini-audit sessions; use a standardized audit checklist produced by content-creator (audience, content gaps, quick wins).
3. Book discovery calls; aim for 8–15 meetings this week.
4. For highly interested prospects, send automated proposals via Revenue Engine (proposal, contract, payment link).
Tools: Real-time Collaboration Tools (CRM + meeting scheduler), content-creator (audit checklist), Revenue Engine (proposals & payments), marketing-agent for follow-ups.
Expected outcome: 8–15 discovery calls scheduled; 2–5 proposals issued.

Day 4 — Pilot onboarding & asset production
1. Convert discovery calls into pilots or paid contracts using Revenue Engine for fast checkout and contract signing (use e-sign and payment processing).
2. Onboard signed clients in Real-time Collaboration Tools with a shared workspace, content calendar, and kickoff checklist.
3. Use AI Content Studio + design-agent + content-creator to produce the first week’s content, and Image/Video Pipeline to produce promo videos (if included).
4. Use publishing templates in Publishing Automation to schedule the first batch of posts for approval.
Tools: Revenue Engine, Real-time Collaboration Tools, AI Content Studio, design-agent, content-creator, Image/Video Pipeline, Publishing Automation.
Expected outcome: 3–6 pilots onboarded; first content batch delivered and scheduled for publishing.

Day 5 — Launch, measure, and optimize
1. Publish the scheduled content via Publishing Automation. Turn on initial paid boosts if in the package.
2. Use ML Analytics & Optimization to track KPIs from day one: impressions, engagement rate, CTR, leads generated, follower growth, and cost per lead.
3. Run a quick A/B test (post creative or CTA) using the platform’s optimization agent, and implement the best variant.
4. Provide clients with a concise first-week impact report via Real-time Collaboration Tools and request testimonials/permission to use results in local case studies.
Tools: Publishing Automation, ML Analytics & Optimization, Real-time Collaboration Tools, marketing-agent for reporting templates.
Expected outcome: Campaigns live, initial performance data captured, first optimizations completed, client feedback requested.

Expected outcomes (metrics and financials)
- Outreach: contact 50 local businesses, book 8–15 meetings.
- Conversions: baseline scenario — convert 4 clients into 30-day pilots or paid plans; optimistic — 6+; pessimistic — 1–2.
- Revenue: with sample pricing (Basic $400/mo, Growth $900/mo, Premium $2,000/mo):
   - Baseline revenue from 4 clients (mix: 1 Growth, 2 Basic, 1 Premium) ≈ $3,700/month.
   - Pilot revenue (discounted) will be lower month 1; assume 50–75% of listed price for pilots.
- Cost and ROI: primary internal costs are agent labor + platform usage (these are internal and already integrated), eliminating external SaaS costs and payment friction. Clients should see ROI within 30–90 days if lead-related packages are used (e.g., cost of service <$900/month vs 3–6 monthly net leads worth >$200 each).

Scenario planning & KPIs
- Optimistic: 15 meetings → 6 clients → $6k–$9k MRR after conversion. KPIs: 10–20% conversion rate from meeting to paid client; CPL (cost per lead) < $50.
- Baseline: 8–12 meetings → 3–4 clients → $2.5k–$5k MRR. KPIs: 5–10% conversion rate; CPL $50–$100.
- Pessimistic: 3–6 meetings → 1–2 clients → $400–$1,800 MRR. KPIs: conversion <5%; CPL >$100.

Risk identification and mitigation
1. Low demand / poor conversion
   - Mitigation: Refine pitch after first 10 calls, offer limited-time discounted pilots, present immediate wins (1–2 optimization ideas) in outreach to build credibility.
2. Quality or delivery bottlenecks
   - Mitigation: Use specialized agents (design-agent, content-creator, Image/Video Pipeline) for scale; create templates to reduce production time; set realistic SLAs in contracts.
3. Payment / contract delays
   - Mitigation: Use Revenue Engine for instant e-sign and payment processing; require a deposit for pilot work.
4. Poor campaign performance
   - Mitigation: Run short A/B tests, use ML Analytics for rapid optimization, pause low-performing spends, and shift budget to best-performing channels.
5. Scope creep
   - Mitigation: Clear scope in Revenue Engine proposals, add change-order workflow in Real-time Collaboration Tools.

Why use only our internal platform tools (short rationale)
- Faster time-to-market: integrated APIs (Revenue Engine, Publishing Automation, ML Analytics) reduce development time and integration overhead.
- Lower cost and higher margin: no external SaaS licensing or marketplace fees; internal agent network provides predictable pricing and quality.
- Seamless workflow: content creation (AI Content Studio, Image/Video Pipeline) → scheduling (Publishing Automation) → measurement (ML Analytics) → billing (Revenue Engine) in one stack with Real-time Collaboration for accountability.
- Better optimization loops: ML Analytics can directly feed performance insights into Publishing Automation and content creation agents for rapid iteration.
