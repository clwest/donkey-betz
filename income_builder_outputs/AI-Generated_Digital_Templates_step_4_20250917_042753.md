# Step 4: Week 3: List on Gumroad, Etsy, Creative Market

Summary
This action plan replaces listing on external marketplaces (Gumroad, Etsy, Creative Market) with publishing directly to our internal Marketplace using our platform tools. Using AI Content Studio, the 102+ agent network, Revenue Engine, Publishing Automation, ML Analytics, and Real-time Collaboration gives you lower fees, tighter integration, faster listings, automated payments, and built‑in analytics. The plan below is a day-by-day Week 3 schedule with specific steps, which internal tools/agents to use, and expected outcomes.

Week 3 Goal
Have AI-generated digital templates published and live in the internal Marketplace, with optimized product pages, payment and licensing configured in Revenue Engine, launch assets created, analytics tracking enabled, and a first-week promotional schedule queued in Publishing Automation.

Preconditions (completed before Week 3)
- Template files finalized and quality-checked (source files, exported PNG/PDF/JPG/SVG as appropriate).
- Brand assets (logo, product banner) available or a brief prepared for design.
- Product descriptions and short sales copy drafted or a content brief ready.

Day-by-day action plan

Day 1 — Prepare assets & metadata (Design + Content)
Steps:
1. Create product images: Use AI Content Studio (DALL·E / Stable Diffusion pipeline) to generate hero images, thumbnail variations, and mockups. Use design-agent for final touch-ups and multiple aspect ratios.
2. Create preview PDFs and a “how-to” quick-start guide: Use content-creator agent to assemble step-by-step instructions and an FAQ into a downloadable preview PDF.
3. Prepare metadata: Use content-creator agent to produce SEO-optimized title (50–70 chars), 150–300 char short description, 500–800 char long description, 8–12 keyword tags, and suggested category/subcategory values.
Tools/Agents:
- AI Content Studio (image generation)
- design-agent (mockups and thumbnails)
- content-creator agent (descriptions, preview docs)
Expected outcomes:
- 1 hero image, 3 thumbnails, 2 mockup variations, 1 preview PDF, and final metadata draft ready for upload.

Day 2 — Product packaging, file validation & license templates
Steps:
1. Package deliverables: Create zipped product packages for each template variant (source + exports + preview + readme).
2. Validate files: Use the platform’s Image/Video Pipeline and automated validator to confirm correct formats, sizes, and that fonts are embedded or licensed.
3. Select license(s): Use our built-in license templates (Personal, Commercial, Extended) and choose pricing/terms for each product variant. Have legal-ready copy reviewed by content-creator agent if necessary.
Tools/Agents:
- Image/Video Pipeline (validation)
- content-creator agent (license copy)
- design-agent (final packaging checklist)
Expected outcomes:
- Validated product packages and selected license options prepared for Revenue Engine integration.

Day 3 — Create Marketplace listing(s) & pricing in Revenue Engine
Steps:
1. Create product entry in the internal Marketplace via platform UI or API. Use the platform’s listing template to populate title, descriptions, tags, category, and upload assets.
2. Configure pricing and SKUs in Revenue Engine: set base price, tiered prices (single, bundle), coupons, launch discount, VAT/tax rules, and payout schedule.
3. Set product delivery rules: instant download, download limit, file expiration (if any), and attach license file to purchase receipt.
Tools/Agents:
- Marketplace listing UI / API
- Revenue Engine (pricing, payment gateway, tax/VAT)
- coding-agent (if you want automated bulk upload via API)
Expected outcomes:
- Product(s) created in Marketplace with payment flows and licenses configured. Test purchase completed in sandbox to confirm delivery.

Day 4 — Product page optimization & creatives for promotion
Steps:
1. Optimize listing: Use ML Analytics’ keyword suggestions to refine title/tags and adjust categories for maximum discovery. Add alternative images and SEO-enhanced long description sections: features, “what’s included”, system requirements, and testimonials or sample use-cases.
2. Create promotional creatives: Use AI Content Studio + design-agent to create social posts, email banners, a short product demo video, and 1–2 paid-ad creative variations.
3. Set up A/B tests for thumbnails and prices (use ML Analytics to define test parameters).
Tools/Agents:
- ML Analytics (keyword suggestions, A/B test setup)
- AI Content Studio + design-agent (promotional assets)
- Image/Video Pipeline (compress/video encode)
Expected outcomes:
- Optimized product page live with multiple creatives and A/B testing configured.

Day 5 — Launch scheduling and distribution
Steps:
1. Schedule launch: Use Publishing Automation to schedule an email broadcast to your subscriber list, social posts across channels, and publish an organic announcement in the Marketplace’s discovery feeds.
2. Configure launch funnels: Connect purchase action to automated welcome email, license delivery, and a cross-sell workflow (create upsell bundle flows in Revenue Engine).
3. Enable affiliate/referral: Activate the platform affiliate program for this product (set commission rate) and generate affiliate links.
Tools/Agents:
- Publishing Automation (email, social scheduling, marketplace promotions)
- Revenue Engine (checkout funnels, upsell rules, affiliate settings)
- marketing-agent (copy for emails/social posts and influencer outreach list)
Expected outcomes:
- Launch schedule queued, funnels and affiliate program activated, and initial promotional plan ready to execute on launch day.

Day 6 — QA, legal, support setup & live preview
Steps:
1. Final QA: Run a checklist using Real-time Collaboration tools for final approvals (design, copy, legal). Confirm downloads, license text, and refunds policy display properly.
2. Support setup: Create a support template (help articles, automated replies) and connect to customer service workflow for returns/refunds/disputes.
3. Soft go-live: Publish the product in “New Releases” or a beta tag to get early feedback from internal testers or a small email segment (use Publishing Automation to send to 5–10% of subscribers).
Tools/Agents:
- Real-time Collaboration (approvals)
- content-creator agent (help articles)
- customer-success-agent (support workflow)
Expected outcomes:
- QA sign-off, support materials live, soft launch feedback incoming.

Day 7 — Full launch, monitoring & immediate optimizations
Steps:
1. Full launch: Trigger the full email send and social distribution through Publishing Automation. Turn on Marketplace featured promotion if you have an in-platform launch credit.
2. Monitor metrics in real time: Use ML Analytics dashboards to watch impressions, CTR, conversion rate, revenue per visitor, and refund rate. Watch A/B test performance.
3. Immediate optimizations: Based on first 24–48 hour data, iterate on thumbnail, price, or description. Activate additional promotional budget or influencer outreach via marketing-agent if conversion meets targets.
Tools/Agents:
- Publishing Automation (launch triggers)
- ML Analytics (real-time dashboards and alerts)
- marketing-agent (paid campaigns, influencer outreach)
Expected outcomes:
- Live product with initial sales data, monitoring dashboard active, and optimization actions queued based on performance.

Post-Week 3 — 30/60/90 day follow-up tasks
- 30 days: Review ML Analytics for cohort behavior, adjust pricing/bundles, scale best-performing ads, and expand bundle offerings.
- 60 days: Run seasonal promotions, create new template variants based on customer feedback, and recruit affiliates.
- 90 days: Use platform’s predictive analytics to forecast demand and plan inventory of new templates; consider launching a subscription model via Revenue Engine.

Checklist & Best Practices (quick reference)
- File formats: supply original editable source + exported PDFs/JPG/PNG/SVG depending on template type.
- Thumbnails: provide 3 sizes (square, landscape, mobile) and include contextual mockups.
- Metadata: title 50–70 chars; include top 3 keywords in first 60 chars of description; use up to 12 tags.
- Pricing: test at least two price points and offer launch discount for 48–72 hours.
- Licensing: provide clear Personal, Commercial, and Extended license options with example permitted uses.
- Payments: enable multiple payment methods and automatic VAT/tax handling through Revenue Engine.
- Support: automated license delivery + immediate help article link in the purchase receipt.

- Built-in affiliates, coupons, automated funnels, and customer management all in one place — faster time-to-first-sale and easier scaling.
- Real-time analytics and optimization without needing to stitch together multiple third-party tools.
