# Step 5: Week 4: Promote on social media

Overview
This Week 4 action plan focuses on promoting your AI-Generated Digital Templates across social channels using only our internal platform tools. The plan covers content creation, scheduling, paid testing, tracking, and optimization. It uses our AI Content Studio, Image/Video Pipeline, 102+ Specialized Agents (marketing-agent, design-agent, content-creator, video-agent), Publishing Automation system, Revenue Engine for sales links and coupons, ML Analytics & Optimization for tracking, and Real-time Collaboration Tools for approvals. This approach reduces cost and complexity because everything is produced, published, paid-for (payments), and measured inside one integrated platform and via our APIs.

Goals & KPIs (Week 4)
- Awareness: impressions and reach across channels. Target: 5,000–25,000 impressions (adjust to your audience size).
- Engagement: likes, saves, comments, shares. Target engagement rate: 1–4%.
- Traffic: clicks to product landing page. Target CTR: 0.8–3% from social posts.
- Conversions: purchases, email captures. Target conversion rate: 1–4% from social traffic.
- Measurables (set in ML Analytics): impressions, clicks, CTR, add-to-cart, purchases, revenue, CAC, ROAS.

Roles & Internal Tools
- AI Content Studio (DALL·E/Stable Diffusion) — hero graphics, thumbnails, template mockups.
- Image/Video Pipeline — short demo videos, reels, animated GIFs.
- design-agent — layout and branded image preparation for each asset type.
- video-agent (from agent network) — edit and format short-form videos per channel.
- content-creator agent — captions, CTAs, hashtags, and microcopy for posts and ads.
- marketing-agent — audience segmentation, campaign setup, ad budget allocation, targeting strategy.
- Publishing Automation system — scheduling, post distribution, social platform integration, boosted-post setup.
- Revenue Engine — product purchase links, coupons for promotions, embedded checkout, tracking-enabled links.
- ML Analytics & Optimization — real-time dashboards, conversion tracking, A/B test measurement, optimization recommendations.
- Real-time Collaboration Tools — approvals, comment threads, and final sign-off.

Pre-week setup (prior to Day 1)
1. Confirm target personas and priority channels with marketing-agent.
   - Output: 2–3 priority personas and 3 priority channels (e.g., Instagram Reels & Feed, TikTok, LinkedIn).
2. Create or confirm product landing page and Revenue Engine setup.
   - Tasks: ensure product SKU, pricing, coupon code (e.g., LAUNCH15), and checkout are live.
   - Output: purchase links with UTM templates (automatically created via Revenue Engine API).
3. Connect social accounts to the Publishing Automation system and ensure permissions are set.

Detailed Day-by-Day Plan (7 days)

Day 1 — Asset creation sprint
- Use AI Content Studio to generate hero images and template preview variants.
  - Prompt design-agent to produce 6 hero images across brand colorways and template mockups.
- Use Image/Video Pipeline + video-agent to draft 3 short demo videos: 15s product preview, 30s how-to, 10s social teaser.
- content-creator drafts caption library: 9 caption variations (3 for awareness, 3 for features, 3 for conversion), plus 12–15 hashtags, and 3 CTAs.
- Deliverables: 6 images, 3 videos, caption library.
- Expected outcome: ready-to-publish asset pack; reduces external design costs and time by centralizing generation.

Day 2 — Creative polish and approval
- design-agent refines assets for channel specs (Feed, Stories/Reels, TikTok, LinkedIn).
- Use Real-time Collaboration Tools to review and approve assets with stakeholders; track feedback and revisions.
- marketing-agent crafts 2 ad creative sets using best-performing caption & asset pairings.
- Create UTM-tagged links in Revenue Engine for each campaign and add coupon codes for A/B testing.
- Expected outcome: approved creative, two ad variants, tracking-ready links.

Day 3 — Organic launch & baseline measurement
- Schedule organic posts via Publishing Automation:
  - Post 1 (Morning): 15s product teaser (Reel/TikTok) + awareness caption and link.
  - Post 2 (Afternoon): Carousel/Feed image showing 3 template use-cases.
  - Post 3 (Evening): LinkedIn post focusing on productivity/business use-case.
- Configure ML Analytics event tracking: social_click, landing_view, add_to_cart, purchase, coupon_used.
- Expected outcome: initial impressions, early engagement, baseline traffic metrics captured in ML Analytics.

Day 4 — Paid test launch & retargeting setup
- marketing-agent launches two small-budget paid experiments via Publishing Automation’s ad module (integrated with platform ad management):
  - Ad Set A: Awareness — 1 creative, broad lookalike/custom audience, $200 budget.
  - Ad Set B: Conversion — 1 creative, retargeting recent engagers, $200 budget.
- Set campaign pixel & Revenue Engine checkout events for conversion attribution automatically.
- Configure retargeting audiences in marketing-agent (e.g., video viewers, landing page visitors).
- Expected outcome: initial ad impressions, clicks to landing page, measurable early conversions.

Day 5 — Engagement push & influencer micro-outreach
- Publish community engagement content via Publishing Automation (polls, question stickers, short tutorial clip).
- Have the influencer-agent (specialized agent) contact 3 micro-influencers from our vetted list for shoutouts or story features; propose a revenue-share/coupon partnership via Revenue Engine (unique coupon links).
- Launch an Instagram Stories limited-time promo (24–48 hours) using Revenue Engine coupon LAUNCH15.
- Expected outcome: increased social proof, micro-influencer reach, spike in traffic via coupon tracking.

Day 6 — Analyze & iterate
- ML Analytics & Optimization runs morning report: compare ad sets, creatives, audience segments, and channel performance.
- Based on insights, marketing-agent swaps underperforming creative, increases budget on effective ad set, and adjusts targeting (e.g., switching to higher-converting lookalike).
- content-creator drafts follow-up posts highlighting best social proof (screenshots of happy customers, demo clips).
- Expected outcome: measurable lift in CTR and conversion rate; improved ROAS on winning creatives.

Day 7 — Scale & next-week planning
- Scale winning ad set budgets incrementally via Publishing Automation and marketing-agent controls.
- Schedule a follow-up organic post with a case study or customer testimonial; include a CTA to the Revenue Engine storefront.
- Use ML Analytics to assemble a Week 4 performance dashboard and recommendations for Week 5 (e.g., top channels, creative that drove conversions, audience segments to expand).
- Expected outcome: improved ROI from focused spend, clear actions for Week 5 (e.g., scale influencer partnerships, expand ad creative variants).

Tracking and Measurement (ongoing)
- Use ML Analytics & Optimization dashboards to monitor:
  - Impressions, reach, and engagement by post and channel.
  - Clicks to landing page (social_click), add-to-cart, purchases, and coupon redemption by UTM.
  - CAC and ROAS by campaign and creative.
- Set daily alerts for major performance shifts (e.g., CTR drop >30%, conversion spike).
- Use A/B test results (creative, caption, CTA) to automatically suggest next best actions.

Retargeting & Funnel Follow-ups
- Use audience lists created in marketing-agent for:
  - 0–7 day engagers: retarget with conversion-focused creative.
  - 7–30 day site visitors: retarget with testimonial + limited-time offer.
  - Cart abandoners: send incentive coupon via email or social DM (Revenue Engine coupon integration).
- Use Publishing Automation to sequence retargeting posts and ads.

Deliverables by end of Week 4
- Content pack: 6 hero images, 3 videos, caption bank of 9 variations, hashtag pack.
- Two paid ad sets with UTM-tagged links and coupon tracking.
- Published organic content across selected channels and influencer outreach initiated.
- ML Analytics Week 4 performance dashboard with recommendations.
- Documented content calendar and scaling plan for Week 5.
