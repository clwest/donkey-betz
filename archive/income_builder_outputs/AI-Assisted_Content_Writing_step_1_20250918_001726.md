# Step 1: Week 1: Create profiles on Upwork, Fiverr, Contently

Summary and reframing
You asked to create profiles on Upwork, Fiverr, and Contently for an AI-Assisted Content Writing offering. Rather than using external marketplaces, the fastest, most cost-efficient, and better-integrated approach is to create equivalent presences inside our platform: (1) a Creator Marketplace profile, (2) a Professional Portfolio/Showcase page, and (3) a Service Listing(s) backed by the Revenue Engine. This keeps branding, payment, proposals, analytics, and content generation fully integrated and reduces fees, duplicated work, and ongoing maintenance.

Below is a detailed Week 1 action plan (daily steps), the internal tools/agents to use, and the expected outcomes and acceptance criteria.

Week 1 objective
Create and publish a high-converting Creator Marketplace profile, a professional portfolio page, and three service packages for the AI-Assisted Content Writing opportunity — all implemented using internal platform tools and integrated with payment, automation, and analytics.

Day-by-day action plan (Week 1)

Day 1 — Strategy & assets kickoff
- Tasks:
  - Define target clients (e.g., SaaS blogs, e-commerce product pages, B2B newsletters), pricing goals, and top 3 service packages (e.g., AI-augmented blog posts, landing page copy + A/B variants, newsletter content + sequencing).
  - Request brand assets: logo, color palette, hero image, fonts.
- Internal tools/agents:
  - marketing-agent to run a target-client brief and positioning worksheet.
  - design-agent to generate starter brand assets (logo + cover images) using AI Content Studio for variations.
- Deliverables:
  - One-sentence positioning statement, buyer personas, and a folder with 3-5 brand asset variations (logo, hero image, thumbnail).
- Expected outcome:
  - Clear target market and initial visual assets ready for profile and portfolio.

Day 2 — Profile copy & headline
- Tasks:
  - Draft profile headline, short bio, long bio, value proposition, services list, and SEO keywords.
  - Create trust elements: brief credentials, process overview, turnaround times, and sample policies (revisions, refunds).
- Internal tools/agents:
  - content-creator agent to produce multiple headline and bio variations optimized for conversion and search inside the platform.
  - legal-agent (if available) to review a short refund/revision policy.
- Deliverables:
  - 3 headline options, 3 short-bio options, and final long-bio with keywords.
- Expected outcome:
  - Completed, optimized profile copy that’s ready to paste into the Creator Marketplace and Portfolio page.

Day 3 — Portfolio generation (sample work)
- Tasks:
  - Generate 3 portfolio pieces that showcase the AI-assisted process (e.g., “AI + human-edited blog post”, “Landing page + variant”, “Email sequence with AI prompts and human polishing”).
  - Produce accompanying case notes that explain the AI-assisted workflow and outcomes (time saved, SEO improvements, conversion focus).
- Internal tools/agents:
  - AI Content Studio to create draft content pieces (use DALL·E/Stable Diffusion for any visuals or featured images).
  - content-creator agent to edit, humanize, and add case notes and results.
  - design-agent to format portfolio pages/screenshots into visual tiles.
- Deliverables:
  - Three polished portfolio items (content + featured image + short case note).
- Expected outcome:
  - Portfolio demonstrates capability, shows workflow, and answers client questions before contact.

Day 4 — Package creation & pricing
- Tasks:
  - Define three service packages (Basic / Standard / Premium) with deliverables, turnaround, number of revisions, and add-on options.
  - Configure pricing tiers, discounts for monthly retainers, and any setup fees.
- Internal tools/agents:
  - Revenue Engine to model pricing, set up packages, enable payment options (one-time, subscription, retainer), and create contract templates.
  - marketing-agent for suggested price positioning vs. competitors in your target vertical.
- Deliverables:
  - Three live service packages in the platform’s Service Listing area with clear deliverables and terms.
- Expected outcome:
  - Packages configured in Revenue Engine, with payment and contract flow ready.

Day 5 — Profile assembly & QA
- Tasks:
  - Build the Creator Marketplace profile and professional Portfolio page using assets, copy, packages, and samples.
  - Add FAQs, response templates, and a short video/voice introduction (optional).
  - Set up canned responses and lead intake form (questions to qualify leads).
- Internal tools/agents:
  - Creator Marketplace UI (internal) to enter profile details and publish.
  - content-creator agent to produce canned proposal templates and lead intake questions.
  - AI Content Studio to produce an optional 30–60s intro video or explainer image.
- Deliverables:
  - Published Marketplace profile + Portfolio page; canned proposal templates saved.
- Expected outcome:
  - Live profile discoverable in internal marketplace search; intake form captures lead data.

Day 6 — Integration & automation
- Tasks:
  - Connect profile to Revenue Engine for checkout, invoicing, and payout settings.
  - Set up ML Analytics tracking to capture profile views, conversion-funnel metrics, and lead sources.
  - Create automated workflows: new lead → auto-response → proposal sent → scheduled call.
- Internal tools/agents:
  - Revenue Engine to confirm payment methods, invoices, and contract automation.
  - ML Analytics to create a dashboard for profile KPIs.
  - automation-agent (or publishing automation system) to configure email/SMS workflows and calendar integration.
- Deliverables:
  - Payment & contract flow enabled; analytics dashboard and automation flows live.
- Expected outcome:
  - New leads will trigger standardized workflows and revenue flows with immediate tracking.

Day 7 — Launch promotion & review
- Tasks:
  - Publish an announcement post (platform feed, email to existing prospects, or cross-channel social push) to drive initial traffic.
  - Schedule A/B tests for profile headline and package descriptions.
  - Review Week 1 deliverables, set KPIs for Week 2 (e.g., first 10 profile views, 3 qualified leads).
- Internal tools/agents:
  - publishing automation to schedule announcements across internal channels and integrated social/email accounts.
  - marketing-agent to set up A/B tests and initial paid/internal promotion plan (if desired).
  - ML Analytics to define KPIs and set alerts.
- Deliverables:
  - Announcements scheduled and launched; A/B test plan live.
- Expected outcome:
  - Measurable initial traffic to the profile and the first set of leads within 72 hours.

Checklist / Profile fields to complete (practical)
- Headline (value + niche): e.g., “AI-Assisted B2B Blog Posts — Faster, SEO-Ready, Human-Finished”
- Short bio (1–2 lines)
- Long bio (process, guarantees, credentials)
- Services offered (3 packages + add-ons)
- Portfolio items (3)
- Pricing and delivery times
- FAQs and revision policy
- Intake form questions
- Welcome / auto-response template
- Payment / contract connected via Revenue Engine
- Analytics dashboard (views, conversion, avg. time to hire)

KPIs and expected outcomes by end of Week 1
- Primary outcomes:
  - Live Marketplace profile and Portfolio page published.
  - Three polished portfolio items reflecting AI-assisted workflow.
  - Three service packages configured in Revenue Engine with payments/contracts enabled.
  - Automated lead intake and canned proposal templates deployed.
  - Analytics dashboard tracking profile views, lead rate, conversion funnel.
- Success metrics (first-week targets):
  - Profile published and discoverable (100% complete).
  - 3 portfolio pieces live.
  - Payment and contract automation tested and active.
  - Baseline analytics established: profile view tracking, lead capture, and at least one automated workflow triggered.
  - 0–3 inbound leads (real numbers depend on promotion; promotion scheduled using publishing automation).

Why use our internal tools (short justification)
- Seamless integration: profile, portfolio, payments, proposals, and analytics all operate under the same platform API with one central dashboard, eliminating manual reconciliation.
- Cost savings: no external marketplace fees or duplicate subscription costs; Revenue Engine handles payments and invoicing natively.
- Faster iteration: AI Content Studio + content-creator agent can quickly produce/test portfolio items and copy; ML Analytics gives immediate feedback for optimization.
- Automation & scale: publishing automation and built-in agent workflows reduce time to respond and improve conversion rates.

Next steps for Week 2 (brief)
- Monitor analytics, iterate profile copy (A/B headline/test), and begin outreach to 10 target prospects via platform channels.
- Use ML Analytics to refine audience targeting and pricing.
- Start collecting client testimonials or initial case-study metrics.
