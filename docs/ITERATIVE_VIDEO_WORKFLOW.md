## 🔥 The Iterative Video Workflow

### Stop Fighting for One Perfect Video - Iterate to Perfection!

```
Step 1: GENERATE Perfect Static Image
  Tool: Image Studio (Generate tab)
  Goal: Get the PERFECT static image first
  Example: Purple T-Rex in disco outfit with perfect details

  ↓

Step 2: ANIMATE with Image-to-Video
  Tool: Video → Image-to-Video (with GPT-5 enhancement ✨)
  Goal: Bring static image to life with movement
  Example: "T-Rex disco dancing with exaggerated movements:
           arms waving side to side, hips swaying dramatically..."

  ↓

Step 3: EXTEND with Video-to-Video
  Tool: Video → Video-to-Video (with GPT-5 enhancement ✨)
  Goal: Add more frames, extend duration, smooth interpolation
  Example: Extend from 5 seconds to 10 seconds with smoother transitions

  ↓

Step 4: ENHANCE with Upscale Video
  Tool: Video → Upscale Video
  Goal: 4K quality, better resolution, professional polish
  Example: Upscale 512x512 animation to 4K cinematic quality

  ↓

Step 5: PERFORM with Character Performance (if character)
  Tool: Video → Character Performance
  Goal: Make character talk, react, perform with audio
  Example: Make T-Rex lip-sync to disco music
```

## 🎬 Video Sections with GPT-5 Enhancement

### ✅ Text-to-Video
- **When to Use:** Creating video from scratch without static image
- **GPT-5 Helps:** Transform vague descriptions into cinematic prompts
- **Example:** "sunrise" → "Golden sun rising over mountain peaks, rays breaking through clouds..."

### ✅ Image-to-Video
- **When to Use:** Animating your perfect static images (PRIMARY USE!)
- **GPT-5 Helps:** Add specific body part movements, sequences, camera work
- **Example:** "T-Rex dancing" → "T-Rex with arms waving side to side, hips swaying dramatically..."

### ✅ Video-to-Video
- **When to Use:** Extending, interpolating, or modifying existing videos
- **GPT-5 Helps:** Describe modifications with specific directional guidance
- **Example:** "add more movement" → "Increase arm swing amplitude, add head tilts..."

### 🔧 Upscale Video (No prompt needed)
- **When to Use:** Enhancing resolution after animation is perfected
- **Technical operation:** 4K upscaling with AI enhancement

### 🎭 Character Performance (No prompt needed)
- **When to Use:** Making characters lip-sync or perform with audio
- **Technical operation:** Audio-driven animation
