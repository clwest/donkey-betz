### R1 — Benchmark Provenance

The benchmark corpus is NOT merely a taxonomy validation set.

Adopt the following benchmark hierarchy:

- **Priority 1:** Real operational queries harvested from actual platform work.
- **Priority 2:** Observed failure cases.
- **Priority 3:** Synthetic queries only to fill identified coverage gaps.

Every synthetic query should identify which coverage gap it exists to fill.

The benchmark should remain representative of actual platform usage rather than an artificial classifier exercise.

### R2 — Gate Philosophy

Per-family gate values are **STARTING PRIORS**.

They are **NOT** optimization targets.

Phase-0 exists to discover what accuracy is realistically achievable.

If evidence demonstrates the proposed thresholds are inappropriate, Phase-0 is expected to recommend revised thresholds rather than optimize toward the originals.

### R3 — Context-Neutral First

Execute Phase-0 using **query text only**.

Do NOT incorporate conversational context, active workspace, current task, or other contextual signals into classifier decisions.

Instead:

- Measure how often context would have changed the correct routing decision.
- Every such occurrence becomes a first-class observation.

The objective is to answer:

> "Can routing be inferred from the query alone?"

before increasing architectural complexity.

### R4 — Context Needed Classification

"Context Needed" is an acceptable scientific outcome.

It is **NOT** a routing failure.

Record it separately from:

- CONFIDENT
- AMBIGUOUS
- UNCLASSIFIABLE

The experiment should explicitly measure:

- how often context is required,
- what type of context,
- whether the missing context was conversational, operational, or repository state.

Do not solve this during Phase-0.

Only measure it.

### R5 — Wrong-but-Plausible Priority

Continue treating "wrong but plausible" as a distinct failure mode.

This should receive equal or greater attention than obvious retrieval failures because it is operationally more dangerous.

### R6 — Architecture Discipline

Phase-0 exists to answer a question.

It does NOT exist to validate today's proposed architecture.

If evidence suggests:

- taxonomy collapse,
- taxonomy expansion,
- relationship collapse,
- new relationship types,
- abandonment of routing,
- or a fundamentally different model,

those outcomes are considered successful results.

**Architecture follows evidence. Evidence does not follow architecture.**

Recommended promotion candidate for Playbook amendment (2 triggers needed per §20 two-triggers rule; S2822 = trigger 1).
2. Synthetic-heavy benchmark corpora inflate confidence in classifier-shaped systems. Any future retrieval/routing/classification arc should apply R1 provenance discipline before drawing architectural conclusions from aggregate metrics.
3. The research process (evidence-forward per R6) prevented a potentially wrong architectural conclusion. This is the strongest single validation of the routing-first vs fusion-first methodology arc that began at S2821.
