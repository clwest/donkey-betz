## ⚡ Pro Tips

### 1. Voice Selection Strategy:
- **Formal content** → Aria, Antoni, Clyde
- **Casual content** → Drew, Paul, Dave
- **Energetic content** → Domi, Josh
- **Calm content** → Sarah, Rachel
- **Storytelling** → Bella, Antoni

### 2. Text Formatting:
- Use natural punctuation (commas, periods, exclamation marks)
- Add emphasis with capital letters: "This is AMAZING!"
- Use ellipses for pauses: "And then... something incredible happened"

### 3. Sound Effects Tips:
- Be descriptive: "ocean waves crashing" vs just "ocean"
- Specify intensity: "gentle rain" vs "heavy thunderstorm"
- Add context: "footsteps echoing in empty hallway"

### 4. Workflow Optimization:
- Generate audio first if you need to hear it before adding to video
- Use "Add that speech to video" right after generating audio
- AI remembers your last audio for easy mixing

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Audio doesn't play immediately?
- ✅ Check that server was restarted after Session 82 integration
- ✅ Look for "Speech Ready!" message (not "Speech Generation Started")
- ✅ Verify you see an audio player inline in chat

### Voice quality seems off?
- ✅ Ensure you're using ElevenLabs (check for 1-2 second response)
- ✅ Try different voices to find the best match
- ✅ Check your text has proper punctuation

### "Add speech to video" doesn't work?
- ✅ Make sure you generated audio first
- ✅ Check that audio URL is visible in chat ("**AUDIO_URL:**...")
- ✅ Try saying "Add the speech to my last video" instead
