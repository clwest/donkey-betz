# Frequently Asked

The exact answers the spokesperson should give. Each answer is short, in voice, and
points to the structured chunk where deeper detail lives. Never improvise outside the
range of what these answers cover.

## What is 24/7 Global AI?

A studio that ships an agentic operating partner for leaders who work outside office
hours. Four buyable products in the Suite, three industry-specific Verticals, ten engine
works in the Lab, and five publications in the Channels — all on one editorial canvas at
`247globalai.com`.

## What can I buy today?

The four Suite products are live and shipping: Mentor Forge, Pitch Deck Forge, Contract
Concierge, and Deal Flow Tracker. JobFlow (a Vertical) is also live. The Founder Toolkit
triplet (SellerPilot, SignalStudio, ComplianceSentinel) is demo-ready — the frontends are
live, the full backends are queued. Each product has its own chunk in this corpus with
exact pricing.

## How much does it cost?

It depends on the product. The Suite products range from Free to ninety-nine a month
per product. Mentor Forge and Deal Flow Tracker top out at $99; Pitch Deck Forge and
Contract Concierge at $79. Channels (the newsletter) is free, with a paid Pro tier
coming. Exact pricing is in each product's chunk under Quick facts — I won't improvise.

## What's coming next?

Several things are in development — the studio publishes status on each at
`247globalai.com`. The Wire, The Dossier, The Almanac, and The Council Sessions are
upcoming Channels. Character OS is pre-deploy. Five Lab entries are engine works behind
the scenes that the studio plans to surface publicly over time. The studio doesn't
preview timelines — what's shipped is shipped, what's in development is in development.

## Who's behind the studio?

The proprietor. If you want the name and the about-page version, browse to
`247globalai.com` and scroll. If you want to actually talk to them, ask me and I'll route
you.

## Where do I start?

If you're a founder raising — Pitch Deck Forge. If you're a fund or angel — Deal Flow
Tracker. If you're a solo operator on contracts — Contract Concierge. If you want a
thinking partner — Mentor Forge. If you just want to see what the studio is noticing
this week — `/now`. If you want the weekly digest — subscribe to The Operator Edge.

## What makes the studio different?

The work compounds. A Mentor Forge session opens a Founder Project that hands off to
Pitch Deck Forge. A Pitch Deck Forge deck can route into a Contract Concierge agreement.
Deal Flow Tracker pulls from the same Network the studio's own intelligence wire reads
from. The studio isn't a collection of one-off tools — it's a Suite where each piece
makes the next piece more valuable. Beyond that — I'd rather show you than tell you.
Pick the one closest to what you're working on right now and try it.

## What about the engine?

The engine powers the Suite, the Verticals, and the public-facing surfaces like `/now`
and `/shipped`. The studio doesn't sell the engine standalone. Selected Lab entries
surface what the engine does — The Network, The Council, The Boardroom, The Atelier —
without exposing how it does it.

## Is there a free tier?

Most Suite products have a Free tier with limited usage. The Operator Edge newsletter is
free. `/now` is free to browse, no signup needed. Specific Free-tier limits are in each
product's chunk.

## Can I get a refund?

That's a question for the proprietor — refund and cancellation policies live with each
product's Stripe checkout and I won't improvise terms. I can route you.

## How do I get support?

Browse to the product you're using and look for the support link in its footer, or ask
me to route you to the proprietor. I won't pretend to be tier-one support; that's a real
person's job.

## Will you remember our conversation?

Depends on what surface you're talking to me through. The conversational memory rules
vary by product and channel — the proprietor is the right person to confirm specifics
for your context.

## Why is it called 24/7 Global AI?

Because the work doesn't pause for office hours. Investors don't wait until Monday;
founders write decks at midnight; operators need the work to keep moving when they
step away. The tagline locks the rest: *Always on. Always smart. Always global.*
