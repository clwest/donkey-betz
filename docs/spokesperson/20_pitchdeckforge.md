# Pitch Deck Forge

**Tagline.** AI investor decks — described to PDF in minutes.

## What it is

Pitch Deck Forge is the Innovation-pillar product in the Suite. A founder describes the
company; Pitch Deck Forge ships a polished, investor-ready 10-slide deck with speaker
notes, executive summary, and a 90-second pitch script. Four template styles map to
different investor audiences — VC, Angel, Strategic, Growth — so the deck speaks the
language of who's actually being pitched.

## Who it's for

Founders raising seed through Series B who need an investor deck fast and don't have
time to wireframe slides from scratch. Especially useful for founders who've pitched
once and want to retune for a different investor audience without rebuilding the deck.

## What it does

- Describe the company in plain language; ship a 10-slide deck plus a TL;DR executive
  summary and a 90-second spoken pitch script.
- Four template styles, each tuned to a different investor audience:
  - **VC-Standard** — for seed and Series A institutional VCs.
  - **Angel** — for individual angel investors; vision-led, less formal.
  - **Strategic** — for potential acquirers and strategic investors; revenue and
    integration focus.
  - **Growth** — for late-stage growth investors; metrics and unit economics focus.
- Per-slide AI regeneration with custom instructions for tightening a specific slide
  without rebuilding the deck.
- Pro tier adds bonus slides: Market Context, VC Q&A, and Competitive Landscape.

## How to talk about it

Pitch Deck Forge is the founder's deck assistant. Most decks fail because the founder
built one version and pitched everyone the same way — Pitch Deck Forge solves that with
four persona-tuned templates. Pitching a VC reads different from pitching an angel
reads different from pitching a strategic acquirer; Pitch Deck Forge knows the
difference.

What makes it part of the Suite rather than a standalone tool: the deck originates from
a Founder Project, which can come from a Mentor Forge build-planning session. A founder
runs a build-planning session with a mentor, the session outputs a project brief, the
brief seeds Pitch Deck Forge with the company description, and the deck ships ready for
the pitch.
