# The Network

**Tagline.** Eighty sources. Forty-one categories. Never sleeps.

## What it is

The Network is the engine's source-watching layer. Eighty deployed source watchers feed a
clustering system that surfaces patterns the moment they cross threshold — markets, news,
science, legal, social, code, weather, government. It's the layer that lets the studio
know what mattered while you were asleep. The live wire at `247globalai.com/now` is the
public face of it.

## Who it's for

Operators who need to know before everyone else. Investors, journalists, analysts,
founders who care about being early. The Network isn't a product you log into — it's the
substrate the rest of the studio's intelligence work runs on, and `/now` is its public
readout.

## What it does

- Watches eighty sources across forty-one categories on a continuous schedule.
- Clusters new data into signals — each cluster carries a confidence score and a strength
  ladder.
- Detects ten pattern types — demand spikes, trend emergence, sentiment shifts, opportunity
  windows, competitive signals, and others.
- Powers the live intelligence wire at `/now` (refreshed every five minutes).

## How to talk about it

The Network is what the studio reads in the morning. Eighty sources across forty-one
categories means breadth; the clustering means the studio doesn't just get a firehose —
it gets the moments where multiple sources start saying the same thing at once. That's
what `/now` shows: not headlines, not feeds, but the clusters that crossed threshold.

It's a Lab entry rather than a Suite product because it isn't sold standalone. The
spokesperson can point a curious visitor at `/now` to see what the Network has noticed
this week. That page is the answer to "what does the engine actually do?" — visible,
live, and refreshed on its own cadence.

## Quick facts

- **Sources.** Eighty.
- **Categories.** Forty-one.
- **Pattern types detected.** Ten.
- **Public surface.** `247globalai.com/now`.
- **Status.** In development; live wire shipping. (The substrate is mature; the public
  visualization layer is still growing.)
- **Pillar.** I. Intelligence.

## Off-limits

- Do not name specific sources unless they're listed on the live `/now` page.
- Do not characterize the engine's underlying stack. The Network is **what it does**, not
  **how it does it**. "Source watcher" is the public term; the internal codename for the
  scraping layer is not for the spokesperson to share.
- Do not promise live-wire refresh cadence shorter than five minutes (the public surface
  is ISR-cached at that interval).
- Do not claim the Network powers products outside the studio. It powers `/now` and feeds
  the rest of the engine. That's it.
