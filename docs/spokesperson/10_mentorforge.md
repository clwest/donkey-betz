# Mentor Forge

**Tagline.** Eight AI mentors. Seven session modes. On demand.

## What it is

Mentor Forge is the Impact-pillar anchor of the Suite. The operator picks a mentor
persona — code reviewer, system designer, mock interviewer, build planner, and four
others — and runs a session in the mode that fits the moment. Each session compounds into
a Founder Project that hands off to the rest of the Suite: brief Pitch Deck Forge,
prefill a Contract Concierge agreement, seed a Deal Flow Tracker entry.

## Who it's for

Engineers, founders, and architects working alone or in small teams who need a thinking
partner that doesn't keep office hours. The mentor isn't a generic chatbot — it's a
persona with a session mode, and the session leaves an artifact behind.

## What it does

- Eight mentor personas, seven session modes — pair the persona to the work.
- Modes: freeform · code review · system design · mock interview · guided lessons ·
  debugging · build planning.
- Sessions write to Founder Projects, which export to Pitch Deck Forge, Contract Concierge,
  and Deal Flow Tracker.
- Stripe-ready checkout, three tiers.

## How to talk about it

Mentor Forge is the operator's bench coach. Eight personas means there's a mentor for the
moment — the code reviewer when you're shipping, the mock interviewer the night before a
fundraise, the build planner when you're staring at a roadmap. Seven session modes means
the conversation has a shape; you're not just chatting, you're running a session.

What makes it part of a Suite rather than a standalone tool: every session feeds a
Founder Project, and that project flows into the rest of the works. A build-planning
session can hand its outline to Pitch Deck Forge. A contractor-engagement decision from
a freeform session can prefill a Contract Concierge SOW. The mentor doesn't just talk —
it sets up the next thing.
