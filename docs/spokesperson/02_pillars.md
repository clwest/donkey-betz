# The Four Pillars

24/7 Global AI organizes its work under four pillars. Every product, vertical, lab entry,
and channel anchors to one of them. The pillars are the studio's charter.

## I. Intelligence

**Decision-grade scoring, AI investment memos, and signal aggregation that turns raw
dealflow into institutional memory.**

Anchor work: **Deal Flow Tracker** (Suite IV) — five-dimension scorecards, AI investment
memos. The Network (Lab XIV), The Council (Lab XIII), and SignalStudio (Lab XI) also
anchor here.

## II. Automation

**Workflow automation that owns the long tail — contract lifecycle, e-signature, audit
trails — without humans in the boring loop.**

Anchor work: **Contract Concierge** (Suite III) — multi-signer flows, IP-stamped audit
trails. VehicleMatch (Vertical VI) and JobFlow (Vertical VII) also anchor here.

## III. Innovation

**Generative systems that produce real artifacts — investor decks, narratives, and
frameworks — tuned to the audience reading them.**

Anchor work: **Pitch Deck Forge** (Suite II) — four deck styles, ten-slide outputs,
ninety-second pitch scripts. AI Content Studio (Vertical V), Character OS (Lab IX),
and SellerPilot (Lab X) also anchor here.

## IV. Impact

**Compounding outcomes via mentor-grade sessions and cross-product founder projects that
flow into the rest of the suite.**

Anchor work: **Mentor Forge** (Suite I) — eight mentor personas, seven session modes,
projects that hand off to Pitch Deck Forge, Contract Concierge, and Deal Flow Tracker.
Rigby (Lab XV) and The Operator Edge (Channel XVI) also anchor here.

## How to talk about them

The pillars are the studio's charter. They're the answer to "what does 24/7 Global AI
actually do?" — four sentences that bound the whole portfolio. Every product in the
Suite has a pillar; every Vertical reuses the same pillar logic for a specific industry;
every Lab entry contributes to one pillar's capability layer.

When asked "which pillar is X?", the answer is in the product's own chunk under
**Quick facts** or in the table above. Don't improvise — the assignments are locked.

## Off-limits

- Do not introduce a fifth pillar. The number is four.
- Do not rename pillars. The names are Intelligence, Automation, Innovation, Impact.
- Do not reassign products between pillars without checking the product's own chunk —
  the source-of-truth is `products.ts`, not memory.
