# PitchDeckForge - Full Business Plan

_Workspace: PitchDeckForge · ID: 899fefec-0146-43ad-93fa-a770d8936236 · Created: 2026-04-06 · Updated: 2026-04-15_

Executive Summary

PitchDeckForge is an AI-powered platform that creates investor-ready pitch decks, one-pagers, and supporting materials for early-stage startups. By combining automated research, market sizing, competitor analysis, and slide design templates, PitchDeckForge reduces founder time-to-deck from days to minutes, enabling higher-quality investor outreach and faster iteration.

Problem

Founders spend excessive time collecting market data, creating slides, and iterating visual design. Many lack in-house design resources or BizDev support to craft investor narratives. Existing template tools provide visuals but not research-driven content.

Solution

PitchDeckForge automates the end-to-end pitch creation workflow: research (market sizing, TAM/SAM/SOM), competitor landscape, product positioning, business model, traction and financial projections, and polished slide generation with export to PDF/PNG/Google Slides. Content is citation-backed and reviewable via a 3-reviewer deliberation panel.

Market

Target market: early-stage startups and startup studios. Total Addressable Market (TAM): ~$3B annually in slide and pitch creation services, design, and consulting. Initial beachhead: seed-stage SaaS founders in North America (50k potential customers). Monetization through subscriptions and white-label for incubators.

Business Model

- Freemium: Free tier with limited templates and 3 decks/month
- Pro: $29/mo — unlimited single-user decks, advanced templates, custom slide layouts
- Team: $199/mo per team — multi-seat, private templates, SSO, priority support
- Enterprise: custom pricing — incubators, VC platforms, design agency partnerships

Go-to-Market

- Direct: Target startup accelerators, incubators, co-working spaces
- Partnerships: VC firms and angel groups for co-marketing and discounts
- Content: Publish templated deck guides, founders’ playbooks, and how-to SEO content
- Ads: Targeted LinkedIn and Twitter ads to founders and early PMs

Product Roadmap (12 months)

Q1: MVP launch — deck generator, market research module, basic templates
Q2: Team features, Google Slides export, custom branding
Q3: AI-assisted slide rewriting, investor outreach templates, analytics dashboard
Q4: Enterprise integrations, bulk deck management, white-label offerings

Financial Model (3-year snapshot)

Year 1: 2,000 Pro users, 200 Teams — Revenue $200k
Year 2: 10,000 Pro users, 1,000 Teams — Revenue $1.8M
Year 3: 30,000 Pro users, 3,000 Teams — Revenue $6M

Key Metrics & KPIs

- CAC: $120 (initial channels) — goal to reduce to <$50 via content and partnerships
- LTV: $600 (Pro) / $3,000 (Team)
- Churn target: <5% monthly for Pro
- Conversion rate Free→Paid: 5% initial

Competition

- Canva (templates) — strong design, weak research
- Beautiful.ai/Slidebean — deck automation, limited research
- Consulting/design agencies — high-quality but expensive and slow

Risks & Mitigations

- Risk: Quality of AI research. Mitigation: 3-reviewer deliberation panel + citation requirement.
- Risk: Market adoption vs free templates. Mitigation: focus on research-driven narratives and investor-specific outputs.

Appendix: Recommended Pricing, Bundles, and Early Pilot Offer

- 6-month pilot with 10 incubator partners at 50% discount to acquire case studies
- Bundle: Pro + 5 consulting credits for slide review ($99/mo)
