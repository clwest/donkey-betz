# MentorForge — Investor-ready Business Plan

_Workspace: MentorForge · ID: 76421180-37cc-476e-98ac-5a648075e863 · Created: 2026-04-06 · Updated: 2026-04-15_

Executive Summary / Elevator Pitch:
MentorForge is a two-sided marketplace connecting ambitious mentees (early-stage founders, junior engineers) with experienced mentors for short, high-impact paid mentorship sessions (30–90 minutes) and asynchronous feedback. MentorForge helps mentees accelerate learning and projects while giving mentors a flexible monetization channel.

Target Market & Customer Personas:
- Primary: Early-career software engineers and junior PMs seeking career growth and practical advice.
- Secondary: Early-stage founders and indie makers needing tactical mentorship.
- Personas: Junior Engineer (needs resume/interview help), Early Founder (needs product/GT M guidance), Mentor Coach (experienced engineer wanting side income).

Revenue Model / Pricing Tiers:
- Pay-as-you-go: 20% platform fee on session price (mentor sets price).
- Subscription: Mentee Plus $29/mo — 2 discounted sessions/month + priority booking.
- Mentor Pro: $19/mo + 10% platform fee — perks: analytics, priority leads.
- Enterprise / University partnerships: bulk credits, cohort programs, placement dashboards.

Competitive Landscape & Differentiation:
- Competitors: MentorCruise, Clarity.fm, GrowthMentor, LinkedIn Mentorship programs.
- Differentiators: Short-session focus, tight scheduling UX, curated mentor vetting, session notes + action checklist, integrated ratings and micro-feedback loop, marketplace incentives for frequent mentors.

Go-to-Market Strategy:
- Launch to developer communities (GitHub, dev.to, Hacker News), university career centers, and startup cohorts.
- Partnerships with bootcamps and accelerators to seed mentors and mentees.
- Offer initial mentor recruitment incentives (reduced fees for first 3 months) and credit-based discounts for mentees.

Marketing Strategy:
- Social media: Twitter/X (product stories, mentor highlights), LinkedIn (case studies), Reddit (r/learnprogramming, r/startups) for organic engagement. Posting frequency: LinkedIn 3x/wk, Twitter 5x/wk, Reddit AMAs monthly.
- Newsletter: Bi-weekly mentorship insights + success stories; gated signups via free session raffle. Cadence: Bi-weekly.
- Content marketing: Blog: "How to structure a 30-minute mentorship session", "Mentor spotlight"; SEO keywords: mentorship for engineers, book mentor sessions, find a mentor engineer.
- Launch plan: Beta cohort (invite-only) → public launch with partnerships and PR in developer outlets.

Growth Metrics & KPIs:
- GMV (gross marketplace volume), take rate, MRR (subscriptions), conversion rate (visitor→booked session), DAU/MAU, retention by mentee cohort, mentor activation rate (signed up → first session), average session price.

6-month Roadmap:
Month 0–1: Seed mentors (50), build referral program, finalize payments integration (Stripe Connect).
Month 2: Launch public beta, start partnerships (bootcamps), implement subscription tiers.
Month 3: Add mentor analytics dashboard, review moderation tools.
Month 4: Implement Stripe payouts & tax docs, improve matching algorithm (skill-weighted).
Month 5: Launch University/Enterprise pilot and cohort features.
Month 6: Expand to additional geos, start paid acquisition (Meta/LinkedIn ads).

Financial Projections (Conservative / Moderate / Aggressive for 6 months):
- Conservative: 1,000 sessions booked total at avg $30/session; platform takes 20% → revenue $6,000.
- Moderate: 5,000 sessions at $35 avg → revenue ~$35,000 + subscriptions $5k/month by month 6.
- Aggressive: 20,000 sessions at $40 avg + 2,000 subs → revenue ~$180k.
(Assumptions: CAC $30 via developer community outreach, gross margin 80% after Stripe/infra.)
