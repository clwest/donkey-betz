# DealFlowTracker — Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
DealFlowTracker is a modern, lightweight deal-pipeline CRM built for angel investors, micro-VCs, and accelerator programs that need a streamlined, evidence-driven workflow for sourcing, evaluating, diligencing, and closing early-stage investments. The product combines a public founder submission form, a Kanban pipeline tailored to investor workflows, a five-dimension scorecard for consistent deal qualification, AI-generated investment memos, and a fulsome activity log for auditability and collaboration.

- Built to replace spreadsheets, email threads, and ad-hoc notes with a single source of truth.
- Rapid screening: reduce time-to-decision by 40% for early-stage deals through structured scorecards and AI summarization.
- Monetizable via seat-based plans and premium diligence features with an expected SaaS gross margin >80%.

## 2. Problem & Opportunity
Angel investors and small venture firms typically manage deal flow with spreadsheets, Gmail, Dropbox, and manual notes. This fragmented stack creates several pain points:

- Low signal-to-noise in sourcing: high-volume submission streams with inconsistent formats require manual triage.
- Inconsistent evaluation: different partners apply ad-hoc criteria leading to missed opportunities or poor prioritization.
- Inefficient diligence: gathering documents, tracking conversations, and consolidating notes is time-consuming.

Market opportunity: there are approximately 320,000 angel investors and over 2,000 micro-VC funds in the U.S. alone. If we target a conservative SAM of 10,000 small institutional investors and accelerators worldwide and capture 5–10% penetration within five years, DealFlowTracker could reach 500–1,000 paying organizations. With an expected average revenue per organization of $1,200–$6,000/year depending on tier, the revenue opportunity is in the low-to-mid tens of millions ARR within 5 years.

## 3. Product & Features
DealFlowTracker is designed to be usable on day one and powerful enough to scale with more sophisticated diligence workflows.

Core capabilities:

- Public Submission Form
  - Customizable intake form that founders can use to submit pitch decks, traction metrics, cap table summaries, and links. Supports file uploads, external links (Pitch/Notion/Doc), and structured fields (monthly revenue, ARR, net new users).
  - Spam and bot protection, plus source attribution (referral, event, partner).

- Kanban Pipeline
  - Visual board with default stages: New → Review → Diligence → Term Sheet → Won → Lost. Custom stages supported by enterprise plans.
  - Drag-and-drop prioritization, tags, and saved filters (by sector, check-size, geography).

- 5-Dimension Scorecard
  - Structured scoring across Team, Market, Traction, Defensibility, and Fit (each 1–10). Scores roll up to a composite score with configurable weighting.
  - Scorecard templates and macros for common investor theses (SaaS, consumer, hardware).

- AI Memo Generator
  - One-click AI-generated investment memos (3–5 paragraphs + bullet summary) based on submission data, scorecard, and attached materials. Outputs a draft memo editable by partners.
  - Explanations for scores and a highlight extractor that surfaces key traction signals and risks.

- Activity Log & Notes
  - Full audit trail for submissions, comments, score changes, document uploads, and emails. User-level permissions and mention support for investor collaboration.

- Integrations & Exports
  - Export CSVs for LP reporting, Slack notifications on new high-scoring deals, Dropbox/Google Drive attachments, and calendar integration for scheduling diligence calls.

Seed data & sample flows:

- Comes pre-populated with 6 seed deals (representative of SaaS, consumer, biotech, marketplace, hardware, and fintech) to demonstrate scoring and memo generation with realistic fields and attachments.

Security & compliance:

- Role-based access controls, optional SSO for enterprise, data encryption at rest and in transit, and optional NDA gating on sensitive uploads.
