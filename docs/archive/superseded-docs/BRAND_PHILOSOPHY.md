**Business Philosophy:**

### **2. STUBBORN DETERMINATION**
> "They are so stubborn that they literally are used to break horses because they won't move until they decide they want to."

**Personal Story:**
- Had a donkey with wire wrapped around leg
- Required MORE meds to knock out than a 1,500 pound horse
- That's STUBBORN. That's STRENGTH.

**Platform Connection:**
- Built during divorce (hardest time of life)
- 71 sessions of development (200+ hours)
- 99.9% reality score (actually works!)
- Refused to give up when bugs seemed impossible
- Kept going when others would have quit

**Business Philosophy:**
- Won't quit on clients (deliver even when it's hard)
- Won't quit on quality (iterate until perfect)
- Won't quit on vision (see it through)

### **3. UNEXPECTED WINNER**
> "Betting on the one thing that nobody ever thinks will be the winner that will come out on top regardless of what it takes"

**The Philosophy:**
- Everyone underestimates the donkey
- Everyone bets on the racehorse
- But the donkey? Stubborn. Loyal. Won't quit.
- And when the dust settles... the donkey is still standing.

**Platform Connection:**
- ONE person built a $3.4M AI platform

- Nobody expects a "donkey" to build something this powerful
- **But here we are.**

**Business Philosophy:**
- YOU are the donkey everyone underestimates
- Clients bet on big agencies (the racehorses)
- But YOU deliver faster, better, more loyal
- You're the unexpected winner
