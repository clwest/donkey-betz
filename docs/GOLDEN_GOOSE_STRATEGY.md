## Keeping Your Secret Weapon Secret

### Rules for Staying Anonymous About The Tech:

1. **Never Mention AI** (to clients/customers)
   - Say: "Our creative process"
   - Not: "Our AI system generates..."

2. **Emphasize Your Expertise**
   - "I've developed a proprietary workflow"
   - "Efficient production pipeline"
   - "Streamlined creative process"

3. **Show Process Strategically**
   - Show them refinements you make
   - Show the thinking behind choices
   - Don't show the 50 options you generated in 10 minutes

4. **Use "We" Language**
   - "We can deliver in 24 hours"
   - "Our team has this ready"
   - They assume you have staff

5. **Deliver Through Professional Systems**
   - Branded email (you@donkeycreative.com)
   - Professional invoicing
   - Client portal for deliverables
   - Looks like a real agency

6. **Competitive Advantage Protection**
   - Your speed is your moat
   - Your quality is your reputation
   - Your secret is your superpower
   - Don't give it away
