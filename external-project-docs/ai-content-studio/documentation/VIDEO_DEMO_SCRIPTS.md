## 🎬 Filming Tips

### Setup Requirements:
- **Screen Recording**: High-quality screen capture (1080p minimum)
- **Audio**: Clear microphone for voice demos
- **Lighting**: Professional lighting for talking head segments
- **Background**: Clean, professional background or branded backdrop

### Editing Guidelines:
- **Pace**: Keep it fast-paced, especially for social media
- **Text Overlays**: Highlight key statistics and benefits
- **Music**: Upbeat for social media, professional for enterprise demos
- **Branding**: Include AI Content Studio logo and colors consistently

### Call-to-Action Strategy:
- **Social Media**: "Link in bio for early access"
- **YouTube**: "Link in description to join the waitlist" 
- **Enterprise**: "Schedule a demo at [website]"
- **All Platforms**: Create urgency with "Limited early access spots"
